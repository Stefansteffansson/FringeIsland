# Quotes — a collection for future use

**Purpose:** Quotes, sayings and lines that Stefan wants to keep for later. They could become Whisp lines, breadcrumbs, season or episode seeds, or just a mood. The quotes are stored as they are, with no analysis. Any analysis happens in a discovery session, and that session links back here.

**This is NOT:** canon, a statement, or a decision. Keeping a quote here doesn't mean it will be used.

**How to add one:** add a new entry at the end and continue the numbering (Q001, Q002, …). Never renumber. Give the quote verbatim, the source as far as it is known (write "unknown" rather than guessing an author), the date added, and a few loose tags if they help. If a session has discussed the quote, add a one-line link to it.

---

### Q001 — The butterfly rests in the rain

> Butterflies rest when it rains because they know the water will damage their delicate wings. It's okay to rest during life storms. It's okay, you can rest. The storm will pass and you'll fly again.

### Q002 — What people measure

> I looked in your cup to see if you had enough. You looked in mine to see if I had more than you. That difference is everything. We are not the same. Pay attention to what people measure.

- **Source:** unknown (a widely shared saying)
- **Added:** 2026-09-24, by Stefan
- **Tags:** care vs comparison, measuring, enough, scarcity, relationship
