## Part 2 — Rulings needed

### A. The worlds

**R-01 — Is time an axis of the cosmology?** (blocks the worlds and story chapters)
S31 settled "positive/negative future" as *stake, not coordinate*. S66–S71 make the Whisp the FIM's own future self reaching back from a *possible* future. Unsaid: whether any place is "the future" — whether place 2's beyond is where the future self reaches from and Marath's beyond the collapse.
- **Option 1 (suggested):** the topology has no time axis. The future is the stake carried in the backdrop and reaches the FIM through the Whisp, not through a place. Neither beyond is "the future".
- **Option 2:** the two beyonds are the two futures. This is my pattern from S59 and S66, never confirmed; if you want it, it is a new statement.

**R-02 — Is Marath the dark future at civilisational scale?**
Offered by me (S66 connection b, S70 challenge 2): Marath as Mara at the scale of a civilisation, its beyond the collapse at full strength. Not confirmed. It changes S60's "relative to the FIM" from personal to collective.
- **Option 1 (suggested):** keep open; the bible states only S51, Marath resonates with Mara.
- **Option 2:** confirm it as resonance, not travel: the dark branch resonates with Marath as Mara does, still stake and not a coordinate. Gives S76's "what Marath's beyond really is" its referent.

**R-03 — Fork or continuum?**
S66 says "two ways the future can take"; S31 made the two places two poles of one continuum. Suggested: the future is stated the way the places are, two poles with everything in between, so story never has a binary doom switch (fits S70: no countdown).

**R-04 — "Not possible" (S60): is the beyond unanchorable?**
My reading: no seed holds in Mara undiluted, so a Whisp there hangs on the full cord with nothing to fall back to but the beginning; "not possible" means not survivable for anyone with Mara left. It would close S55's "unsolved mystery" of the fourth rung. Suggested: keep the mystery open, as S55 asks; the bible may mention "possibly unanchorable" as a marked hypothesis only.

**R-05 — Which crossings need a seed?** (blocks the worlds chapter)
Three statements pull differently: S45 (your premise: portals to place 2's far side need seeds; the held resolution: home and village through the ball with no fresh seed, place 3 by seeds; only the near side is anchor-free); S32 (a portal into Marath's *near* side to reach a cause); S40 (field portals to "dangerous space 3" need a threshold seed); S52 (the Gimbal shows both near sides). Suggested: the anchor ladder gates *reaches*, not worlds. Near side of either world: seeing needs the Gimbal, acting needs no seed (the body anchors); Mists can perceive Marath's near side; whether a Mist may *act* there is open. Far reaches of either world not reached through the ball (the wild reaches of place 2 past the village; all of Marath's far side): seed-anchored.

**R-06 — The Shadow's size grows with depth** (S59 was your question; S60 built on it as if settled). Suggested: state it as settled, S59 confirmed by S60.

**R-07 — Depth as exposure, zone per assumption.**
S74's note puts the zones as bands on the Void-distance gradient; S74 challenge 2 says a FIM is in a zone per Big Assumption, not as a whole. Suggested: depth on the cord is the FIM's *exposure* (how much of Mara they let be met); the zone is felt per assumption; no single zone meter, consistent with never labelling order (S58).

**R-08 — The engagement spectrum's third pole "Beyond"** now collides with the reach "beyond".
- **Option 1:** rename the pole (you name it; it means the far end of the expedition way, not "one who goes to the beyond", which S60 says almost no one does).
- **Option 2 (suggested):** collapse to the two ways you named on 2026-09-16, cultivate the home / go on expeditions near or far, and drop the third pole.

**R-09 — Does "the Fringe" survive as a word?** S26 had "the Fringe" as everything past the Shimmer; S63 lifted "FringeIsland" to the universe. Suggested: the Fringe = the in-world name for everything past the Shimmer (both worlds, all reaches); FringeIsland = the universe seen from outside (project, product, and the Ordinary-World frame); the bible says so once.

**R-10 — Danger in the depths: exposure only, or intrinsic?** S27's held said the far reaches are dangerous only because more cord is exposed; S59 gives Marath's depths intrinsic danger (the Shadow grows). Suggested: state both, never "only".
