**What this file is about (Stefan, 2026-09-16, ruling D2):** two different ways of engaging in FringeIsland *as a FIM* — either you cultivate your FIM home at FringeIsland, or you go on expeditions and explore near or far from your FringeIsland home. It is not about the community that creates the FringeIsland experience; for that, see the [roles core](canon--roles--taxonomy.md) (Dreamineer as an authorial mode) and [`canon--community--kickstarter-season-zero.md`](canon--community--kickstarter-season-zero.md).

## Overview

Members engage with FringeIsland along a natural spectrum. No one position is more valid than another. Every path leads to growth. Every position on the spectrum is welcome.

---

## The Spectrum

**Homebody** — tends their inner garden, builds their digital home, reflects quietly, and never ventures far into the wider narrative. This is a genuinely valuable way to inhabit FringeIsland. The Homebody can do deep individual work — the individual perspective (1) — through reflection, journaling, and solo journeys.

**Explorer** — deep in narrative arcs, seasonal episodes, quests, group dynamics, community contribution, world-building. Living in FringeIsland as a genuine second world. Explorers naturally engage the relational and communal perspectives (1+1, 1+community) — the ones that require other people.

**Beyond** — those who press further still, past the village and far out on the cord into the Fringe's deep reaches — the growth-bearing Void distance the world holds but does not yet fully reveal (see the [cosmology core](canon--cosmology--worlds-topology.md)).

Between these: infinite gradations. Members move along the spectrum freely. There is no progression requirement — a Homebody is not "behind" an Explorer.

---

## The developmental honesty

The relational (1+1) and communal (1+community) perspectives cannot be collapsed alone. A member who never engages with others can still grow — but the full developmental arc of FringeIsland opens only through connection.

This is not a judgment of the Homebody path. It is the nature of the model.

The individual perspective can be explored through solo work — reflection, journaling, solo journeys. The relational and communal perspectives structurally require genuine human connection. Not NPC interactions. Not AI-generated activity. Other people.

The island invites, but does not force.

---

## Implications

- The platform must support the full spectrum — solo tools are as important as social features
- Homebody-oriented features (garden, journal, personal reflection) are not lesser or preliminary — they are a complete mode of engagement
- Community and group features are not add-ons — they are the only path to collapsing the relational and communal perspectives
- The cold-start problem (see [Open Questions CQ-001](questions--ecosystem-open-questions.md)) is particularly acute here: relational and communal perspectives need real people

## The member archetypes (Thinking — needs deepening and validation)

*Merged here on 2026-09-16 from `canon--community--member-archetypes.md` (extracted from Hamn PRODUCT_SPEC.md, April 2026; last updated April 14, 2026). Provisional personas: the Homebody and the Explorer illustrate the two poles of the spectrum above; the Dreamineer illustrates the 1+Community perspective and is a mode, not a position on the spectrum. Not yet validated against real members.*

FringeIsland members engage with the world in fundamentally different ways. These archetypes describe three distinct relationships to the platform — not marketing segments, but patterns of inhabitation. A single member may move between archetypes over time, or hold aspects of more than one simultaneously.

These archetypes map onto the three perspectives (1/Self, 1+1/Relationship, 1+Community), though whether this alignment is definitional or coincidental remains to be explored.

### The Homebody (Elena)

**Relationship to FringeIsland:** Tend, reflect, grow quietly.

The Homebody values depth over breadth. Solo journeys, personal reflection, quiet garden cultivation. Community participation is optional and self-paced — she wants to feel at home without pressure to perform. Group-heavy platforms feel overwhelming; solo apps lack warmth.

**Success looks like:** A rich travel log, a growing Whisp, feeling at home.

**Perspective alignment:** 1/Self — the individual dimension of growth.

### The Explorer (David)

**Relationship to FringeIsland:** Dive in, follow the story, connect with others.

The Explorer seeks meaning through structured experiences. Seasonal episodes, narrative arcs, group quests, Whisp encounters. Existing platforms are either too clinical (LMS) or too shallow (social media). He wants something that combines adventure with genuine depth.

**Success looks like:** Sustained engagement across seasons, a deepening Whisp relationship, community belonging.

**Perspective alignment:** 1+1/Relationship — growth through connection with others and with the Whisp.

### The Dreamineer (Astrid)

**Relationship to FringeIsland:** Create, shape, contribute.

A note on the name: in the canonical role taxonomy ([roles](canon--roles--taxonomy.md)), Dreamineer is not a kind of person but the authorial *mode* a FIM occupies — permission-gated, with four specialisations (Creator, Anthropologist, Teller, Wayfinder), each gating a studio. This archetype describes the FIM who is drawn to that mode. She has wisdom and content to share — a coach, educator, creative professional, or passionate community member. No existing platform combines narrative, structure, and community in a way that lets her build meaningful experiences for others. She wants tools that match her creative ambition.

**Success looks like:** Published journeys with active enrollments, positive reviews, sustainable creative contribution.

**Perspective alignment:** 1+Community — growth through giving back to the collective.

### Open questions (archetypes)

- Are these the right three archetypes, or do they need refinement based on real user research?
- Should they have FringeIsland-native names rather than generic labels?
- How do these archetypes inform feature prioritisation — does each wave serve a primary archetype?
- How does a member's archetype evolve over time — is there a natural progression (Homebody → Explorer → Dreamineer)? (Note: roles are modes, not a ladder — movement between them is fluid, per the [roles core](canon--roles--taxonomy.md).)

*The former question "how do archetypes relate to the engagement spectrum" is closed by this merge: the Homebody and the Explorer are the spectrum's two poles, illustrated. These archetypes will deepen as the community forms. The first real members will confirm, challenge, or replace them.*
