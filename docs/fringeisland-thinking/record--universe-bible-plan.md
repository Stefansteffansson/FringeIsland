## Goal

One place where anyone with the right authority can read what FringeIsland **is** and how it is **supposed to work**, today, in the present tense. No history, no "ratified on", no statement numbers in the body. Git keeps the history; the discovery log becomes the workshop where new thinking happens before it is baked in.

The bible contains the backdrop (Session 03), so it is the **deep bible** of CQ-018. A trimmed story bible for authors can be cut from it later.

## Session 06 — the brief

Nine boxes that need only Stefan's ruling. For each: what the bible already says, the options, and Claude Code's recommendation. The recommendations are proposals, not canon; the session rules, and its "Baked into the bible" list carries the answers.

**W-02 — Is Marath the dark future at the scale of a civilisation?** *Already true:* Marath resonates with Mara; Mara scales, the race being a collective immunity to change; the dark future is the stake of the universe, never a whip (§5.7). *Options:* (a) identity — Marath is the dark future at collective size, its beyond the collapse at full strength; (b) resonance only — Marath resonates with Mara at every size, the collective included, while the dark future stays on the backdrop's time axis and Marath is not a place in time; (c) separate — Marath stays personal and interpersonal, and the dark future lives only in the backdrop. *Recommendation:* (b). Identity would make Marath a place in time, which W-10 leaves open, and would put the civilisational stake on every visit to Marath, close to the whip the bible rules out; (b) keeps the rhyme available to Tellers without fixing it.

**W-07 — Nalome's beyond: unmapped by design?** *Already true:* §2.5 says Nalome's beyond "holds nothing named, and is kept unmapped on purpose", and chapter 1 names leaving something unmapped as a principle; the box asks what the body already states. *Options:* (a) confirm — unmapped by design, the box closes, and a later session may open it deliberately; (b) map it in a discovery session. *Recommendation:* (a).

**W-09 — Can anchoring reach the beyond?** *Already true:* three things anchor (the body, the ball, seeds); the beyond is optional and graduation is not gated on it; §2.5's table says "unsolved; possibly unanchorable"; the idea that no seed holds in Mara undiluted is marked as a hypothesis only. *Options:* (a) a mystery by decision — nothing is known in the world about anchoring there, and that is kept, the hypothesis staying marked as one; (b) take it to Fundamentals III. *Recommendation:* (a). Nothing downstream waits on it, and it matches W-07.

**W-15 — Losing or changing the phone, in the fiction.** *Already true:* the Gimbal is the FIM's phone; the ball is one ball in two places, the bottom of the drip in the Tree and attached to the Gimbal; it opens only to its FIM; a physical ball is a keepsake, never a key (§2.3, §2.10). *Options:* (a) nothing is lost — the ball belongs to the FIM, not the device, and returns from the drip to a new Gimbal loaded at sign-in; (b) as (a), with one short rebinding moment in the fiction, the ball finding its new Gimbal, at no cost; (c) the loss costs something in the world, such as the static returning for a while. *Recommendation:* (b), or (a) if no moment is wanted. A mishap in the Ordinary World should not cost anything in the fiction; the human is never the thing at risk (§4.7).

**W-18 — Does the ball mature?** *Already true:* every ball glows equal; the variable signals of a life live on the cord and the lines, never on the ball; the Whisp's filling already scales what a FIM perceives (§2.3, §2.10). *Options:* (a) yes, as capability only, from clunky to fine, never as brightness or a visible rank; (b) no — the ball stays the constant, equal root anchor, and growth shows in the Whisp, the cord and the lines. *Recommendation:* (b). One progression, in the Whisp, keeps "every ball glows equal" simple and avoids a second track that reads as levelling.

**B-11 — The Whisp in genuine distress.** *Already true:* FringeIsland is not a therapy service; in genuine distress the Whisp is honest about its limits and points to human support; tough love never tips a FIM into panic (§3.1, §4.6). *Options:* (a) rule the principles now and make the detail a gate — before any real person meets a Whisp prototype, the detection, the wording and the support resources for each country come from a qualified source (established safe-messaging guidance and a clinical adviser); (b) leave all of it to the Whisp's specification. *Principles proposed for (a):* when distress is suspected, the story and the tough love stop; the Whisp says plainly what it cannot do and points to human help, emergency help included; it never tries to handle a crisis itself; the platform, not the Whisp, owns the thresholds and the resource list. *Recommendation:* (a).

**B-12 — Whether the Whisp names itself as an AI.** *Already true:* the Whisp runs on AI, and the AI is only the medium through which the FIM's own future self speaks; the medium and the one speaking stay distinct in how the Whisp talks (§3.1); the backdrop never attributes the Whisp's origin to the machine (§5.7). A Mist already hears the Whisp while loading the Gimbal, before consent (§2.3), so any disclosure has to come before the Whisp's first word. *The law:* the EU AI Act's Article 50(1) requires that people interacting with an AI system are told so, unless it is obvious; it has applied since 2 August 2026, and the Digital Omnibus did not defer it. The legal reading is [CQ-021](questions--ecosystem-open-questions.md). *Options:* (a) the platform discloses, clearly and before the first dialogue, and inside the fiction the Whisp never denies the medium: asked, it answers plainly that it runs on AI and that the voice is the FIM's own; (b) the Whisp itself names the medium at set moments in its speech; (c) disclosure only in the app's terms and settings. *Recommendation:* (a). (c) likely falls short of Article 50; (b) puts the machine into the Whisp's own voice, against the bible's distinction.

**R-10 — A human mentor or elder.** *Already true:* support roles belong to a group, and more will follow as groups find real use (R-02); "mentor" never means the Whisp; the register's CQ-009 holds the business question of older members as mentors. *Options:* (a) the Guide is enough for now, and R-10 folds into R-02 as a named candidate, CQ-009 beside it; (b) add a Mentor or Elder support role now. *Recommendation:* (a).

**S-16 — Is the Nordic lineage deliberate?** *Already true:* two authored worlds set the quality bar for a coherent world with the uncanny in the everyday, Mats Åkerman's Den Svagiska Unionen and Simon Stålenhag's, both Swedish; none of it is adopted as a mechanic (§5.12). *Options:* (a) deliberate — the Nordic uncanny-in-the-everyday is FringeIsland's chosen aesthetic lineage and guides the design system; (b) one influence among many — the bar is the coherence and the uncanny in the everyday, not the Nordic setting; (c) leave it to the design-system work. *Recommendation:* (b). The near side lies over every FIM's own ordinary world, wherever that is, so a regional look would fight the overlay; the wave names are Norse, but they are planning names, not the world. The minimal design foundation does not wait on this.
