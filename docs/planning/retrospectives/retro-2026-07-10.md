### Learned

- **An adopted slice is a read that already happened — never arm adoption on a page that won't consume it.** `OverviewBoot`'s `BOOT_PATHS` regex also matched group *detail* pages, so a full-load arrival there adopted a groups slice no mount consumed; after a leave/hand-over, the client-side `/groups` mount consumed that **pre-mutation** slice (consume-once) with no revalidation ever firing. Not a race — a stale read satisfying the mount's only read, red the full 15 s retry window. The same latent shape in H023's `ARRIVAL_PATHS` was found and fixed *before* its merge (a deep-linked never-arrived FIM opening a shared group link would have been yanked into the welcome). The class rule is §4 line 1.
- **A ping warms one container; concurrency defeats it.** The L2′ concurrent-pinger experiment failed both ways (pool decay across a 4.5-min gap is a coin flip; a fresh pool still missed 2/4 of the real fan-out), and GitHub's `*/5` cron actually fired 10 times in ~23 h. Pinging retired as a strategy on the evidence, before the migration made it moot.
- **Idle depth is part of any cold number.** The shallow-cold trap produced false "fixed" readings twice before ADR-U043 Amendment 1 pinned the protocol (≥ 20 min enforced idle, depth recorded, 2× tail rule) — enforcement homes already landed (PR #152).
- **Front-door visibility is its own disjunct:** first-arrival enrolment died P0002 for Mist *and* brand-new FIM alike until `enroll_self_in_journey`'s lookup gained `or j.is_onboarding_designated` — out of browse, never out of reach. Sibling lesson: **seed ownership is a visibility decision** — DeusEx (PC014 custodian precedent), because FringeIsland Members' member-disjunct would leak the front door into every browse view.
- **Correct product behaviour can break every fixture:** auto-launch yanked each fresh fixture FIM into the player; `markArrivedOnce` made "arrived once" true by construction across six sibling specs + global-setup, and the transcendence continuity assertion re-anchored to the RESUME — the stronger claim.
- **Vercel deprecated the Edge runtime** (industry-research find) — ADR-U036's premise aged out underneath it; Fluid in-instance concurrency (Node-only) was the actual fan-out fix, exactly the mechanism the research predicted.
