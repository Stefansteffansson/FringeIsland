## What shipped, as a member would tell it (the walkthrough)

When someone writes to me, my inbox notices while I'm looking at it — the conversation rises with its unread mark, the Messages badge ticks up, and if I'm inside that conversation the new message just appears. My group's forum does the same on the group page: a new thread arrives under my eyes, and the half-written reply in my composer survives it; when a moderator removes a post, it becomes the tombstone where it stood. None of this ever *tells* my screen what was said — the platform only whispers *that something happened*, and my Hub goes and fetches the truth through the same doors it always used, doors that still refuse anyone they always refused. If my connection stumbles, the messages surfaces quietly say "reconnecting," everything keeps working, and the moment the socket returns — or I come back to the tab — they catch up. A missed whisper costs me seconds, never a message: the tables were always the truth. Walked against the shipped surface (E2E: two browsers, live DM to a parked inbox + badge, live detail, live forum post + tombstone with the draft intact): every sentence is a tested behaviour.
