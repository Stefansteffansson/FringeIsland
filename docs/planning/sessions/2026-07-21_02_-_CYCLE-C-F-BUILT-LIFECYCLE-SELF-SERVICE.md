## What shipped, as a member would tell it (the walkthrough)

For the first time, leaving is mine. If I need a break, I pause my own account from my profile — one calm confirmation, and my account goes quiet: nothing is deleted, nobody loses what I shared, my groups and walks wait exactly where I left them, and *only I* can turn it back on — the paused surface offers me the way back, and when I take it I land on my groups as if I'd never been away. If an admin suspended me, none of this is mine to touch: pause, delete, and reactivate all refuse — a hold is a hold. And if I want to leave for good, the platform treats that with the weight it deserves: it tells me plainly what will be erased (my journal, my journey record) and what stays with the people I shared it with (my forum posts and messages, shown as "Former member" — my words survive, my name doesn't), offers me my data download before anything irreversible is reachable, and makes me type the words. Then it is done — immediately: my groups see me leave the way the platform always handles departures (a sole-Steward group closes, sealed; co-members keep their group), my private record is erased, my sessions die everywhere, and I get a farewell. If a stale tab tries to wander back in, it meets the honest truth — "This account is closed" — never my old life. Every sentence above is a tested behaviour (2 E2E journeys + the 22-test contract tier beneath).
