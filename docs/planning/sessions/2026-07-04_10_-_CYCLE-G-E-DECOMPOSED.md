## Substrate audit at decomposition (disk + dev DB, 2026-07-04)

G-E's substrate is not merely uneven (G-D's shape) — **parts are live, unspecced, and holed.** The plan's "legacy proves the flows" premise held, but understated the risk; the G-E plan row now carries the correction.

- **Nominated succession is LIVE with a security hole.** `nominate_steward` + `handle_notification_action` + the internal `_handle_stewardship_nomination_action` (sprint3, `20260228125730`) implement ranked-nominee succession (7-day expiry, DeusEx all-decline fallback, ADR-U019). **All three carry `EXECUTE` to `anon` + `PUBLIC`** (confirmed in `pg_proc.proacl` on dev), and `handle_notification_action` **dispatches stewardship side effects on caller-supplied `action_data`** — a direct PostgREST caller (incl. an anon Mist) can drive stewardship changes. Live ADR-U038 hole.
- **The nomination accept-handler carries the G-C `'Member'` bug class, unfixed.** `_handle_stewardship_nomination_action` resolves the Steward role by **`name = 'Steward'` only** (no template fallback). But `create_engagement_group` (PC010, L79-86) names role instances by **template name** — so v2 groups name their Steward role `'Steward Role Template'`, not `'Steward'`. Dev today: **8 legacy `'Steward'` groups + 2 v2 `'Steward Role Template'` groups** (queried). On a v2 group the accept branch resolves no Steward role → nominee granted nothing. Same defect G-C found for `auto_assign_member_role_on_accept` (`name='Member'`).
- **Raw-role-count blind spot recurs.** `nominate_steward`'s sole-Steward check counts `user_group_roles` rows directly — blind to the status-flip blind spot G-D closed with `active_steward_count()`. A paused Steward counts as cover.
- **Two legacy branches were REMOVED at G-D and must be re-landed.** The sprint2 `leave_group` `steward_handover` (sole-Steward→DeusEx) and `group_closure` (last-member) branches were dropped when `leave_group` was replaced in place (PC013); they are the flows the G-D honest refusals point at.
- **Deletion is a raw RLS DELETE with a cascade wall.** `groups_delete` (`delete_group`-gated) permits a client-role hard `DELETE`; but `journeys.created_by_group_id` is `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, so a journey-owning group **cannot be hard-deleted at all**, and where it can, forum authorship + enrolment history are destroyed with no freeze, no reassignment. PC010 deliberately left `groups_delete` for GRP-9.

## Findings worth carrying

1. **The decompose-session substrate audit earned its keep for the third cycle running** — this time by catching a *live security hole*, not just a plan-premise error. The sprint3 nomination surface (anon/PUBLIC grant + caller-data dispatch) is a standing ADR-U038 finding; PC014 STORY-1/STORY-6 demonstrate the anon call red before closing it. Candidate for the **pre-partition SECURITY DEFINER grant sweep** (bridge `_08` finding 4) — this is a second confirmed instance after `leave_group`'s EXECUTE-to-PUBLIC.
2. **The `'Member'`/`'Steward'` name-vs-template resolution bug is systemic across sprint2/3 bodies.** G-C fixed it for invitation accept; G-D's `active_steward_count` used the template+fallback form; G-E finds it again in the nomination handler. Every legacy Steward/role lookup on a bare `name=` predicate is suspect on v2-created groups. Worth a one-time grep at cooldown.
3. **GRP-9 "delete" is genuinely soft-terminal by force of substrate**, not just preference — the `journeys … ON DELETE RESTRICT` FK blocks hard-delete of any journey-owning group. Recommended `archived` tombstone (Open Q5) also keeps forum authorship alive for DS-5/MEM-9. The gate decides; the recommendation is disk-grounded, not aesthetic.
4. **`close_group` (MEM-8) and `delete_group` (GRP-9) keep intents distinguishable via two terminal statuses** — `closed` (ran its course, last member) vs `archived` (deliberately retired, Steward) — both already in the `groups.status` CHECK, GRP-5 renders both.
