## Plain-English walkthrough (the J-B ritual — walked against the shipped behaviour)

A stranger reaches FringeIsland and clicks "Look around." A Mist forms, and instead of an empty landing they find themselves *inside* a short welcome walk — "Arrival on FringeIsland" — in the ordinary player. Nothing holds them there: every navigation link works, they can wander to the journeys list (where their walk now sits, resumable), to the Mist page, anywhere — and no later visit ever throws the welcome at them again, because having arrived once is a fact the platform remembers (the enrolment itself). If they decide to stay — become a FIM mid-walk — they land back in the walk exactly where they left it: same enrolment, same completed steps, nothing restarts. If they drift away instead, the reaper (or their own goodbye) erases the walk with everything else — nothing lingers. A newcomer who signs up directly, never having been a Mist, meets exactly the same door at first sign-in, by exactly the same rule: no enrolment yet means never arrived. *Lifecycle questions asked:* a Mist completing the whole walk before transcending lands on /groups (nothing to resume — unit-tested); a suspended account's arrival check refuses honestly and the landing survives (42501, telemetry, no crash); a deep-linked traveller is never yanked (arrival is a landing, not a page view); re-authoring the placeholder at CQ-010 must keep step structure stable for in-flight walks (hook comment planted in the seed).
