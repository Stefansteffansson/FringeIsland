## The plain-English walkthrough (the bound tier — third application)

*What we built, as a traveller would tell it:* If the group I walked with closes, or I leave it, the journey I lived doesn't vanish — it opens read-only, tells me plainly what happened and when, and everything I did is still there to see. Nothing about my walk is anyone else's to see unless I say so: one switch, plainly labelled, shares my step marks — never my times, never my words — with the group's Stewards and Guides, and flipping it back takes effect at once. A Steward looking at the group's journey sees an honest window: how the walk is going among those who chose to share, said in exactly those words, with everyone listed by name in alphabetical order — no rankings, no times, no one "behind".

*Walked against the shipped behaviour, asking the continuity questions the tiers don't:*
- **Freeze → rejoin → …thaw?** No unfreeze path exists anywhere in the substrate — a member who left, whose self-enrolments froze, rejoins the group and gets back everything membership grants… except those walks stay frozen (View, not Continue), forever. For `group_closed`/`group_archived` permanence is right (the party is gone). For `left_group`/`removed_from_group` + rejoin, permanent freeze is a *choice nobody has made yet*. **Routed to the area gate / the J-E boundary as J-D's honest edge — not fixed in-cycle.**
- **Share → leave → rejoin:** the consent row persists inert while standing is gone (the roster excludes non-members), and on rejoin the old "granted" is still the latest decision — the member is sharing again without a fresh act. Defensible (consent is explicit and revocable; leaving didn't revoke it) but worth one look at the gate: is a standing-loss a consent-pause? **Flagged for the J-O3 look list, not fixed.**
- **Frozen + completed:** holds — the completion framing renders inside the frozen frame; `completed_at` survives (the cascades never sweep completed rows — pinned).
- **The legacy-completed shapes in the panel:** progress derives from instances, never from `enrollment.status` — a legacy walk with no step record shows honestly empty marks, consistent with the J-C walkthrough's edge.
