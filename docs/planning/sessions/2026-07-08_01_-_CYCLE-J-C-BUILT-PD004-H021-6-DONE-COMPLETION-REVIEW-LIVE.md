## The plain-English walkthrough (retro §4's new tier — first bound application)

*What we built, as a traveller would tell it:* I finish the last required step of a journey and the Hub marks the arrival — quietly, with the time I gave it and the span it took, as two honest numbers. Coming back later, the journey opens in review: everything readable, my marks and times beside the steps, nothing recorded just for looking — and the optional or repeatable parts still offer themselves, because finishing is a milestone, not a lock. Finished journeys say *Review* where active ones say *Continue*. If I walk with my group, my finish is mine alone — nobody else's row moves, nobody sees my state.

## Findings / lessons (for the J-C/area retro)

1. **The payload-walk discipline caught a design fault at derivation, not at build** (n=2 for the J-A retro §4 change, n=1 for catching something structural): walking H021's stories against the proposed flip surfaced the guard dead-lock before a single test existed. The Q3 default (loosen to `('active','completed')`) went to the gate with its reasoning attached and survived contact with the whole pyramid.
2. **Append-only substrates make naive erasure simulations impossible — by design, and that's the lesson:** the red suite's cascade proof deleted the personal group directly and hit `consent_records`' ON DELETE RESTRICT + the `enforce_consent_append_only` trigger (42501 outside the controlled path). The honest fix was the real path: `erase_fim_account`, DeusEx-called — the pattern five sibling suites already carry. Erasure proofs should reach for the house erasure functions *first*, never simulate with bare deletes. (ADR-U034 working exactly as intended; the failed simulation was the substrate's success.)
3. **The racing-finals AC was topologically impossible as written:** linear gating totally orders required steps, so "two racing last required steps" cannot exist — the suite races two completes of the *same* final step, exercising the identical row lock. Spec ACs describing concurrency should be checked against the ordering the substrate already imposes.
4. **The delegated shape held at n=2, same friction:** three sub-agent sessions, every claim verified first-hand at the join (suite re-runs, diff audits, suppression counts). Two of three agents needed a ping before delivering their final report (the J-B report-idle friction recurring — now a confirmed pattern for the retro, not an incident).
