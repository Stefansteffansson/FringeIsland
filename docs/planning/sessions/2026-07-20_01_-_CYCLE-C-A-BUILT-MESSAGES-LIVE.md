## What shipped, as a member would tell it (the walkthrough)

I can finally talk to people. Every member on my group's roster has a Message button; clicking it drops me into the one conversation the two of us share — always the same one, no duplicates. Messages sits in my header with a badge counting conversations holding something I haven't read; reading clears it (nothing to do with notifications — there are none for messages, by design). My group's page has a Conversations section now: our Steward opened one, I joined it, said hello, left it, came back — my words were still there under my name. Sending feels instant, and when it fails it says so and offers retry. A Mist sees none of this — no Messages item, no roster buttons, and even a hand-crafted API call gets refused by the platform itself. Walked against the shipped surface: every sentence above is a tested behaviour (integration 21/21 + E2E), and the continuity questions — leave/rejoin history, cross-absence attribution, badge truth after reading — are asserted, not assumed.
