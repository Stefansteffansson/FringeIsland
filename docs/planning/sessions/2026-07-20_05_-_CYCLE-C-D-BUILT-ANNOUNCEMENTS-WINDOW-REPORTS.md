## What shipped, as a member would tell it (the walkthrough)

When my Steward has something the whole group should hear, they say it once — an announcement at the top of our group page, above the forum, with their name on it. I see it when I next look, **even if I joined the group after it was said** — the board doesn't forget, because seeing it is a fact about the group, not about who was around at send time. If it was a mistake, the Steward takes it back and it's gone from everyone's board — though the platform quietly remembers who took it back. When FringeIsland itself has something to say, it's waiting on my groups page, labelled as the platform's word, for every member — and nobody can fake one, because the community door physically cannot write the platform's scope. When I post something clumsy in the forum, I have fifteen minutes to fix it — my post says "(edited)" honestly — or to withdraw it, leaving the same quiet tombstone a moderator would leave; my DMs, as ever, cannot be rewritten by anyone. And when I see something that shouldn't be here — in the forum or in a message — I report it: a reason, a note, done; the platform keeps **what the content said at the moment I reported it**, so a fifteen-minute edit can't launder the evidence; reporting twice just tells me I already did. Nobody but me and the platform's own governance sees my report. Every sentence above is a tested behaviour (E2E 4/4 + the contract and unit tiers beneath).
