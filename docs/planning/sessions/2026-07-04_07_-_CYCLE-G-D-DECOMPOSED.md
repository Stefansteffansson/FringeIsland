Paused members are un-removable on the RLS path.
- **Pause:** `'paused'` sits in the status CHECK with **no write path** (the only UPDATE policy is the self `invited→active` accept) — but the semantics are substrate-given: **`pause_members` + `activate_members` are already seeded** on the Steward template (`seeds/01_permissions.sql:12-13` — the seeds-directory discipline paying out), and `has_permission()` / `get_user_permissions()` resolve **only `status='active'`** (oracled — B-RBAC), so a paused membership goes permission-dark with zero resolution changes and roles survive for reactivation. Both `invited→active` triggers guard on `OLD.status='invited'` — a pause/activate flip fires nothing. **No flow oracle** ("pause/leave have no dedicated lifecycle tests") — MEM-4 specced fresh from canon.
- **Invariant blind spot:** `check_last_leader_removal` fires on role **deletion** and counts **raw role rows** — it can't catch pausing the last Steward (no role write) and counts a paused Steward as cover when removing an active one. PC013 guards **last-active-Steward** contract-side ahead of the untouched walls.
- The AFTER DELETE notify trigger already distinguishes leave (`member_left` → Stewards) from removal (`member_removed` → the removed member) by actor-vs-row; `get_group_detail`'s members payload (PC011-amended: `member_group_id`, `roles[]`) filters `status='active'` and carries no status field.
