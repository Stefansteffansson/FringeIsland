## What Is This

The first beta release of the FringeIsland iOS mobile app — delivered to a limited
number of members with limited functionality. This is a testing and learning
release, not a public launch.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Mobile is how most members will experience FringeIsland day-to-day. Starting the
beta early in Brim gives time to learn, iterate and build toward the full v.1
release in Urd.
