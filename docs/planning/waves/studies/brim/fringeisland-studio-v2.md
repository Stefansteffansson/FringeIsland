## What Is This

Version 2 of FringeIsland Studio adds two new capabilities: members can now
customise their own Garden (previously read-only), and Dreamineers can begin
developing the Void for testing and experimental purposes.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Member agency over their personal space deepens belonging and investment in the world.
Dreamineer access to the Void opens the universe's third dimension for creative development.
