## Mission

Bring the full narrative engine to life with Arc Studio v.1 — giving Dreamineers
the tools to craft seasons and episodes that run across all three dimensions
with the individual member's character always at the centre. Launch the mobile
apps to all members. Continue the natural evolution of all studios. Urd is where
FringeIsland becomes a living, storied universe.
