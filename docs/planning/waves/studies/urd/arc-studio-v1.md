## What Is This

Arc Studio is the narrative authoring tool for Dreamineers to develop seasons
and episodes within the FringeIsland universe. These stories run with the
backdrop of the real world, the Void and FringeIsland — but always with the
individual member's character at the centre.

---

## Why We Are Building This

A living universe needs living stories. Arc Studio is what enables FringeIsland
to evolve narratively over time — through seasons that create shared cultural moments
and episodes that personalise the story to each member's journey.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

*(To be fully developed in concept phase. Directional notes only.)*

- Dreamineers use Arc Studio to design seasons and episodes
- A season has a narrative arc — a beginning, rising tension, climax and resolution
- Episodes are the individual units of narrative within a season
- Stories span all three dimensions (real world, Void, FringeIsland)
- The individual member's character is always primary — the story responds to and reflects the member
- Published seasons and episodes run live for members on the platform

---

## Relationship to Other Studios

| Studio | Scope |
|--------|-------|
| **Arc Studio** | Narrative: seasons, episodes, story arcs, characters |
| **Journey Studio** | Experiential: structured journeys members undertake |
| **FringeIsland Studio** | Environmental: the world, its settings and surroundings |

These three studios are complementary — a season (Arc) may unfold across a journey
(Journey Studio) set in locations shaped by Dreamineers (FringeIsland Studio).
