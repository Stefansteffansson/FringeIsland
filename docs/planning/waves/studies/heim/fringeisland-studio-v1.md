## What Is This

FringeIsland Studio is the authoring tool that gives Dreamineers the power
to shape the FringeIsland environment itself — its settings, surroundings
and the dynamic elements within it. Version 1 is the foundation:
a working tool for managing the island at a platform level.

---

## Why We Are Building This

FringeIsland as a living world cannot be static. Dreamineers need tools to
evolve it — to add new areas, introduce seasonal changes, respond to
narrative developments. FringeIsland Studio is how the world stays alive.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

*(To be developed in study phase.)*

- Dreamineers with the appropriate role can access FringeIsland Studio
- In v.1 the studio covers the FringeIsland environment, settings and surroundings
- Full CRUD operations on the world elements within scope
- Changes in the studio are reflected in the member experience
- In v.1 this is a Dreamineer-only tool — members cannot edit the world
  (member home customisation comes in Brim via v.2)
