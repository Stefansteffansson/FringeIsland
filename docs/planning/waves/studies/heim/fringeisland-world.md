## What Is This

FringeIsland is the imaginary world at the heart of the platform's universe.
It is a place members can reach, explore and inhabit — a destination that
exists in parallel to their real-world journey. This feature covers the
definition, presentation and accessibility of the island and its immediate
surroundings, and how members travel to it.

---

## Why We Are Building This

FringeIsland the place is what gives the platform its name, its mythology and
its emotional core. Without it, the platform is a good tool. With it, the platform
becomes a world members want to belong to.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

*(To be fully developed in the concept phase. Directional notes only.)*

- FringeIsland has a geography, an atmosphere and a set of rules that govern it
- Members have a way of "getting there" — a transition experience
- The island has areas, landmarks or zones that are meaningful in the journey context
- The surroundings of the island (the waters, the approach) are also defined
- The relationship between the real world, the Void and FringeIsland is established here
