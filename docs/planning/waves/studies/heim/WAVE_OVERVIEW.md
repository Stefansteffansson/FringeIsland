## Mission

Introduce the FringeIsland imaginary world — the island itself, its surroundings,
and each member's unique home within it. Deepen the journey experience by connecting
it to the FringeIsland world and expanding the Whisp's role. Give Dreamineers
their first authoring tool for the FringeIsland environment itself.
