## What Is This

A full redesign of the FringeIsland web application using the new design system
and accessibility framework. Every surface built in Ferd and Eid is revisited,
evaluated and rebuilt to the new standard.

---

## Why We Are Building This

The UI built in Ferd and Eid serves a functional purpose during development
but is not the experience FringeIsland intends to deliver. Hamn is the wave
where the platform becomes visually and experientially coherent.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

- All existing screens and flows are inventoried
- Each is evaluated against the new design system and accessibility standard
- A redesign is produced for each surface — first in design tooling, then implemented
- The redesign is not just cosmetic — UX flows are also reviewed and improved
- After Hamn, new UI work starts from the design system, not from scratch
