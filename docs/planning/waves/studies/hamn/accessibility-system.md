# Accessibility System

**Wave:** Hamn
**Category:** Features
**Status:** 🟡 Needs study

---

## What Is This

A systematic approach to making the FringeIsland experience accessible to people
with disabilities. This covers both the standards we commit to and the implementation
that ensures those standards are met and maintained.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Accessibility is not an add-on — it is a statement about who FringeIsland is for.
An immersive edutainment platform for personal growth should be open to everyone.
Building accessibility in at the design system level ensures it is structural,
not cosmetic.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

- A target accessibility standard is chosen (minimum WCAG 2.1 AA)
- All design system components are built to meet that standard
- An audit process is established to verify conformance
- Accessibility is enforced at the component level — not patched at the page level
- Ongoing: new components must pass accessibility requirements before inclusion
