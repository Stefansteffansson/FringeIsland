# Journey Studio v.1

**Wave:** Eid
**Category:** Features
**Status:** 🔴 Needs concept work

## What Is This

Journey Studio is a visual authoring tool for creating and managing journeys.
Version 1 focuses exclusively on journeys in the real world — not connected
to the imaginary world of FringeIsland or the Void. Those dimensions come
in later versions.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Journeys are the core experience unit of FringeIsland. Without a tool to author them,
the platform can only deliver pre-built content. Journey Studio gives Dreamineers
the power to create, shape and evolve journeys — making the platform generative
rather than static.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

*(To be developed during concept and study phases.
The following are directional — not specifications.)*

- A Dreamineer can create a new journey from within Journey Studio
- The studio provides a visual canvas for designing the journey's structure
- A journey consists of stages, steps, prompts or similar building blocks (to be defined)
- In v.1 journeys are grounded in real-world activities and reflections
- The studio supports full CRUD operations on journeys
- Published journeys become available for groups to enroll in
