# The universe discovery queue — the order in which the bible's open questions are taken up

**What this is:** the planning side of the [Universe Bible](../fringeisland-thinking/bible--fringeisland-universe.md). Every Open box in its chapter 8 sits in one row below, in the order the discovery sessions take them up; the two umbrella boxes, W-05 and B-01, are named in their sessions' titles. The bible holds no priorities; this file does. It is kept current at every session's close by Claude Code, by the rule at the end.
**Origin:** the Universe Bible plan's Phase 6, set with Stefan on 2026-09-27; moved here from `docs/fringeisland-thinking/record--universe-bible-plan.md` on 2026-10-01, when the finished plan was deleted (git keeps it, with the plan's goal, Stefan's decisions, the six phases and the Session 06 brief). Discovery sessions run in the Claude.ai project and are appended to Part 3 of [the discovery file](../fringeisland-thinking/discovery--the-universe-in-the-making.md).

## The queue

Set on 2026-09-27 from the bible's chapter 8 as it stood that day; every box placed once. The bible itself stays free of priorities (its chapter 8 says so); the order lives here, in the planning tree.

**How the order is set.**

1. **Cheap first.** Boxes that need only Stefan's ruling are settled in one short sitting, pre-framed with options and a recommendation each, as Session 05 settled R-03 to R-50.
2. **Unblock the most.** Sixteen boxes wait on "the fundamentals"; the standing rule of 2026-06-14 puts mechanics before experience design.
3. **Serve Eid.** Of Eid's three themes, only the Whisp is gated hard by the bible's open boxes. The Journey Studio v1 study is about real-world journeys and needs little from them; the design foundation touches only W-01 and S-16.
4. **Safety before a person meets a Whisp.** B-11 and B-12 are ruled before any Whisp prototype is shown to a real person.
5. **Experience last.** The first hour, the reveal and the founding moment follow the mechanics and the Whisp's specification.
6. **Park** what waits on people, builds or documents that do not exist yet.

**The queue.** Discovery sessions run in the Claude.ai project and continue the numbering (Session 06 opens at S115). Each starts from its row's boxes and ends with "Baked into the bible"; Claude Code carries the answers across.

**Two tracks**, set by the Eid kickoff's open decision on which theme goes first:

- **The Whisp first:** 5, then the fundamentals (2 to 4), then 6 and 7. The queue is on Eid's critical path.
- **Journey Studio v1 or the design foundation first:** 2 to 7 in order, with 9 riding along with the build. No build waits on discovery.

Sessions 8 and 10 to 13 fit between them in any order once what they need first is done; 14 comes last.

**Keeping the queue.** At a session's close Claude Code carries its "Baked into the bible" list across, removes each answered box from its row here as the bible's index line goes, and removes the row once it is empty. A box a session opens is slotted into a row at the same close. A box answered out of order is removed from wherever it sits. When only the parked row is left, this file is deleted; the parked boxes stay in the bible's index with their "waits for".
