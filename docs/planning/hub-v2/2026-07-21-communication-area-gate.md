**Composition correction (empty-cache probe, 22:4x UTC — supersedes the "zero-cache asset load" attribution in the rows above and refines the J-gate convention):** the full empty-cache payload is **266 KB** (11 JS chunks = 204 KB); all assets served `x-vercel-cache: HIT` from the edge, slowest single asset 105 ms, all 18 in parallel in ~0.1 s. The post-TTFB time is NOT asset download — it is **~0.7 s JS parse + hydration (CPU), then the authenticated API waterfall firing only after hydration (~1.3 s per read on the cold function path, in waves)**. Deep-cold is a serialization problem on a cold backend: document (2.7 s) → hydrate (0.7 s) → cold reads (~1.3-2 s+) → render. Consequences: asset optimization has nothing to win; scale-to-one removes both the TTFB floor and the cold-read cost, collapsing the chain to ~1-1.5 s.

**Walk observation (not a rider) — the titleless forum reads as unfinished at the surface.** Scenario 5 surfaced a bare `Start a thread...` box with no title field. **Walk observation (not a rider) — flat 2-level threading is right, but the affordances that make it livable are missing.** Scenario 5 raised the question "why only 2 levels, is that normal?", and the answer matters for the next pass. Two findings. **(1) The invariant has no recorded rationale.** `enforce_flat_threading` pins the depth in the substrate with an oracle-pinned message ("Cannot reply to a reply. Maximum thread depth is 2 levels."), carried in from the v1 build as a strong oracle invariant (B-COMM-004..007) — preserved, never re-argued. The specs record the constraint and its consequences (PD009 §68: "flat threading bounds depth, not width") but nowhere the *why*. An invariant this visible should carry its reason; a walker asked the question the docs couldn't answer. **(2) Stefan's Facebook evidence names the concrete target — and corrects a wrong claim recorded here earlier.** An earlier revision of this observation asserted that "Facebook is structurally the *same* two-level model — a reply to a reply lands in the same flat list, not a third indent". **That is wrong, and Stefan's third screenshot disproves it**: Börje's top-level comment → Mante's reply → Göran's reply *to Mante* render at three distinct indents, each tier carrying its own `Reply to <name>` composer. Facebook nests one level deeper than FringeIsland does. The claim was written from recollection rather than evidence and merged before the evidence arrived — the verify-before-asserting failure mode, recorded here rather than quietly edited away. What the evidence actually shows, across all three screenshots: (a) depth capped, but at **three** tiers, not two; (b) replies **collapsed by default** behind a `View N replies` disclosure — the real answer to the width bound PD009 §68 accepted; (c) an explicit **address** on every reply — composer labelled `Reply to <name>`, body opening with that person's name as a link — used *even at tiers where the indent already says who is being answered*, i.e. structure and address are belt-and-braces, not alternatives. Consequences for the next pass, now honestly separated by cost: collapse and addressing remain **surface-only** (no schema change, no weakening of the depth trigger — the address is content, not structure), and they are the cheap, high-value half. Whether FringeIsland's cap should move from two tiers to three is a **genuine open design question**, not a gap to close by default: it would change `enforce_flat_threading`, the oracle-pinned refusal message ("Maximum thread depth is 2 levels"), and the invariant B-COMM-004..007 pins — substrate work with an ADR-shaped decision behind it, and it must be decided on what a guided group of ~5-15 needs, not on what a public comment section with 227 reactions needs. **Dispositioned by Stefan at the walk (2026-07-22): "note it for future development, we leave our build as is today."** Filed as [`TASK-FORUM-01`](../backlog/tasks/TASK-FORUM-01-reply-addressing-and-collapse.md) — collapse + addressing as the cheap surface half, the depth cap as a recorded open decision, and the missing rationale to be written into FEAT-PD009 either way. No gate-time work; carried to the retro with the titleless-thread question above.
