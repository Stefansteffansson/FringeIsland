# Ecosystem Thinking

**Purpose:** Working ideas, explorations, open questions, and legacy content being mined for useful material. These documents are actively evolving — not yet stable enough for `strategy/` or `universe/`.

**This is for:** Open questions, exploratory documents, legacy files pending extraction, early-stage thinking about topics that will eventually produce strategy documents, universe definitions, or constitutional updates.

**This is NOT for:** Stable directional documents (→ `../strategy/`), universe design that has crystallised (→ `../universe/`), constitutional documents (→ `../VISION.md`, `../MANIFESTO.md`), or research reports (→ `../../research/`).

**Graduation:** When something here matures into a clear position:
- Universe design (how the world works) → moves to `../universe/`
- Strategic direction (how the ecosystem is shaped) → moves to `../strategy/`
- Constitutional change → absorbed into `../VISION.md` or `../MANIFESTO.md`
