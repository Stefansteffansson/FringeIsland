## Why this session, now

Across the universe canon and the architecture descent, almost everything *structural* is firm — the worlds topology, roles, the Whisp, respawn, the studios. But the **first hour has never been designed**, and several things are waiting on it:

- It is flagged in canon as the single highest-risk gap (`../../universe/narrative/README.md` lists "the first experience" as unspecified).
- It is open question **CQ-010** in [`../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`](../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) ("first hour / first experience").
- DS-3 Journeys (`../../../platform/domain/journeys.md`) carries it as its named highest-risk open question — the spec is waiting on this design.
- It sits at the seam of several already-firm concepts (Shadow lifecycle, the Whisp, transcendence, the engagement spectrum, the cosmology entry geometry), so it is now *ready* to design — the pieces it must connect already exist.

This session crystallises the first hour into stable statements that can then graduate into `narrative/` (and/or an ADR, and CQ-010 closes).

## Questions to explore (the design decisions the first hour needs)

These are prompts to open the session, not answers. Stefan drives; Claude captures.

**A. Arrival & the first frame**
- What is the literal first moment — the first screen, the first thing the arrival sees or does?
- Where do they arrive in the cosmology? Do they begin in the Ordinary World, glimpse the Shimmer, stand on the near side?
- What is the emotional hook in the first few minutes — what makes someone stay?

**B. The Whisp's first appearance**
- A Shadow already has a Whisp. When and how does it first speak in hour one? What is its first role — companion, narrator, mirror?
- How does "assessment dissolved into dialogue" actually feel in the opening — how is "Who am I?" surfaced without a quiz?

**C. Shadow → FIM transcendence**
- Does transcendence happen within the first hour, or is hour one entirely a Shadow experience?
- What earns or triggers it, and what is the felt before/after?

**D. Across the engagement spectrum**
- Is the first hour the same for a Homebody-leaning and an Explorer-leaning arrival, or does it fork — and if so, when and how?

**E. Cold-start reality**
- The relational and communal layers need real people. What carries the first hour when the island is sparse (ties to CQ-001)?

**F. Persistence & return**
- Shadow data is ephemeral. If someone leaves mid-first-hour and returns, what persists and what is lost? What is re-entry like?

**G. What it must NOT be**
- The guard rails: no leaderboard/metrics, scaffolding invisible, no didactic "lesson" surface. What would betray the experience?
