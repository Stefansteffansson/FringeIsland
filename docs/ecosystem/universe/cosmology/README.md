# Cosmology — The Three Worlds

**Purpose:** The cosmological frame of FringeIsland — the Three Worlds, their boundaries, transitions, and felt qualities.

---

## Overview

FringeIsland exists across three realms:

- **The Ordinary World** — life outside the platform. Always present, never absent. The departure point.
- **The Safe Harbour** — FringeIsland itself. A place of reflection, sharing, and becoming. Safe, not comfortable.
- **The Other Side** — deeper water. Where narrative, challenge, and transformation live. Not a distant place but an intimate one — each FIM carries their own Other Side.
