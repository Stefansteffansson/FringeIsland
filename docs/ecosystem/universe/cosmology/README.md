## 1. The Ordinary World

Where we act in daily life — work, relationships, ordinary hours (opening frame). It is also the
substrate the Fringe's near sides are read from: the FIM's body never leaves it and never enters
danger, but the body can move, and moving it changes the angle on the Shimmer and which near-side
Fringe is visible from there. The body is a roving vantage into the near side (S27).

## 2. The Shimmer

The membrane/divide between the Ordinary World and the Fringe (S26). An *edge* is a line (the
Shimmer); a *fringe* is the inhabited band beside it (the Fringe) — the project is named for the
band, not the boundary. Perception through the Shimmer scales with how full the FIM's Whisp is:
the Whisp is the faculty of perception, the Gimbal the device, the Shimmer the thing perceived
(S17-18, S26). There is one Shimmer; both places of the Fringe lie behind it (S31).

## 3. The Fringe — two co-located places, one continuum

The whole created universe past the Shimmer. It is two places, both overlaid on the
Ordinary World's coordinates (S31):

- **Place 2** — warm, welcoming; the flourishing the universe is *for*. The FringeIsland place.
- **Place 3** — hostile, cold, dystopian; the anti-flourishing force. As yet unnamed.

They are the two centres of gravity of **one continuous tonal spectrum** (S12, S31) — not sealed
boxes; the "all in between" lives in the contested ground between them. "Positive/negative future"
is *stake*, not coordinate: place 3 is the force that would unmake the bright future, not a
timeline one travels to.

**The near sides are coupled** (S32): the same Ordinary-World coordinates seen two ways. The patch
eating flowers in place 2 and the machine drilling in place 3 are the same wound seen from both
sides. To heal a wound in place 2, a FIM traces it and acts on its cause in place 3 — notice the
blight, cross a portal, stop the cause, watch the warm world heal.

## 4. Near side and the Beyond — the anchor is the gate

Each place has two reaches (S26, S31), and the gate between them is **anchoring**, not
safe-versus-dangerous (S45):

| Reach | Tied to | Anchor | Open to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near side (places 2 and 3) | Ordinary-World coordinates | the FIM's own body | Shadows and FIMs |
| Beyond of place 2 (home, village) | nothing worldly | the ball (permanent root), via the always-on home portal | FIMs only |
| Into place 3 (the deep dark) | nothing worldly | fresh seeds budded from the ball | FIMs only |

Anchor permanence runs **body -> ball -> disposable seeds**. Only the near side is anchor-free,
because reality itself holds you there.

## 5. The Void and the cord

**The Void is an axis, not a region** (S27). It is wherever the Whisp is once it leaves the FIM —
from just outside the skull, along the whole length of the cord, to wherever the Whisp journeys,
in any world. The place topology says *where* the Whisp travelled; the Void is the *fact that it
left* and hangs exposed on the lifeline. Ontologically, the Void is also the primordial state: the
black, un-grown nothingness that place 3 expresses and place 2 is revived from (S34-35). Two
facets, one Void.

**The cord** is the FIM-Whisp lifeline (S27). It runs through the Void for its whole length. If
cut, the Whisp suffers and may die — never the FIM (the Avatar structure, S22). The FIM holds the
dial: the cord runs from FIM to Whisp, and the FIM decides how long it may be — pay out to
venture, reel in to retreat (S40). Risk is depth times protection (cord length times seeding); the
peril is opt-in.

**Two luminosity channels on the cord** (S37): maturity owns salience (vivid early, receding as
the Whisp internalises, S6-7); health owns colour and integrity (whole vs frayed, steady vs
flickering). Cord-health is visible to a friend only along a grown branch — glanceable not
diagnostic, invited not imposed, self first.

## 6. The village, the Tree, and the glowing glass balls

- **The village** (working name, S41) is the safe-harbour commons — the bursting little sea-village
  where FIMs relax, contemplate, socialise. It sits in the **Beyond of place 2** (S33), and it is
  **FIM-only**: a Shadow has no ball, hence no anchor in any Beyond (S45).
- **The Tree** stands in the village, hung with **glowing glass balls**. Each ball is one FIM,
  granted at transcendence (S39). **All balls glow equal** — no one is better or worse than anyone
  else; the variable signals live on cord and branch, never on the ball (S37).
- **The ball is a two-zone gateway** (S42): pressing the **inside** transports the FIM to their
  private home; pressing the **rim** transports them to the village. Centre = inward/private,
  edge = outward/social. The ball absorbs the home portal idea — it *is* the gateway, and it is
  always one step away beside the FIM in the Ordinary World (S33), so the safest place in the
  deepest part of the world is also the nearest refuge.
- **The private home** (S43-44) is a self-chosen representation of where the FIM feels safest — a
  flower field, an attic den, a house above the clouds. Inviolable (default-locked; the FIM holds
  the only key); selectively shareable, whole or parts, granted and revocable. Furnished with the
  personal-scope slice of World Studio, open to every FIM; it evolves as the FIM grows.

## 7. Two bonds: the cord and the branch

Two distinct kinds of bond that must never collapse into one (S36):

- **The cord** — FIM to their *own* Whisp. The inward lifeline; existential stakes (Section 5).
- **The branch** — FIM to FIM. The relational, world-growing bond. Its loss thins the world there;
  it never kills a Whisp.

They interlock: the branch is the channel through which one FIM heals or steadies another's cord —
you can only hold a friend's lifeline along a branch you have grown with them (S27, S36). **The
branches are the visible crown of the Tree** (S38): the social graph is not drawn on the Tree, it
*is* the Tree. Own branches are legible; the wider crown stays ambient (no rankings, no counts).
Branch glow is the relationship's aliveness — a drifted branch thins and waits, recoverable when
tended. Branches are also the routes: following your own branches is how you find your friends
among the balls.

## 8. Anchoring, seeds, and severance

- **Seeds**, budded from the FIM's home ball, are the anchor objects (S40). Planted along a route
  into place 3, each seed is at once climbing-protection and a patch of place-2 life reclaimed
  from the dead ground — anchoring and growing the world are the same gesture.
- **Seed-to-pass:** a danger-portal requires a seed dropped on its edge to cross. The threshold
  seed is the route's anchor-zero and the marked way home; the safe crossings (home portal, the
  village) need no seed; and the rule hard-locks Shadows out, since seeds need a ball (S40, S45).
- **Severance is two-tier** (S27): anchored — fall back to the last anchor and resume; unanchored
  overreach — start over from the very beginning. Recovery is reunion: the FIM and Whisp retreat
  and find each other again.
- **A closing portal** leaves the cord **dead** (seed destroyed: the severe tier) or **stuck**
  (living seed: length frozen, the Whisp moves only within its slack — reopenable, and rescuable
  by a friend along a branch) (S40).

## 9. World-genesis — gardening, not guarding

The warm world is not pre-built and defended; it is **grown** (S35). The balls and the bonds
between them are primary: balls rise from the black nothingness, their bonds weave the crown, the
tree brings the dead ground to life — village, island, all of place 2. **Place 2 is revived
place 3** — the same ground in two states, alive/glowing (tended) versus dead/black (un-grown or
receded). The world is inherited: a newcomer arrives into a crown earlier FIMs grew.

Only each FIM's **own ball is inviolable**; everything else in place 2 is tendable and can recede
toward the Void/place 3 if untended (S34). The governing rule — the most load-bearing in the
cosmology — is **gardening, not guarding**: tending must feel like a beloved garden (gentle,
forgiving, approach-motivated), never like defending a fort. Nothing is permanently destroyed; the
worst case is *not yet grown* or *receded*, recoverable the moment care resumes. This keeps faith
with "Grow is delight, not deficiency" (S28).

## 10. The growth gradient — Void distance, not bodily distance

The comfort -> growth -> panic gradient is spatial (S26), and the distance that matters is **Void
distance** — how far the Whisp has ventured out on the cord — not how far the body walks (S27).
You can stand still and send your Whisp deep, or walk a mile with your Whisp close. "Far from the
safe harbour" means far out on the lifeline. Venture out for challenge, return home for safety:
the developmental breath-rhythm rendered as geography, with meta-safety guaranteed because the
human is never the thing at risk (S19, S22).

## 11. Who can be where

| | Own Whisp + cord | Near side (places 2 + 3) | The village / private home | Deep place 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Shadow** | yes — the Whisp is universal (S39) | yes (body-anchored) | no — no ball | no — no seeds |
| **FIM** | yes | yes | yes (ball, granted at transcendence) | yes (seeds, at chosen risk) |

A Shadow's data is ephemeral (anonymous auth, erased soon after inactivity or close); transcendence
is the persistence-and-consent threshold and grants the ball (S46, S39). The Shadow lock on the
Beyond is intrinsic, not a fence: you do not enter the panic zone before you have a home to return
to.

## What this supersedes

The **Three Worlds** model (Ordinary World / Safe Harbour / The Other Side) is superseded by this
topology. Mapping, so older material reads correctly:

| Old term | Where it landed |
|---|---|
| The Ordinary World | Unchanged (Section 1) |
| The Safe Harbour (a world) | Not a world: the **village** — the safe-harbour commons in the Beyond of place 2 (Section 6) |
| The Other Side (a world) | Not one place: the **Fringe** — two co-located places, each with a near side and a Beyond (Sections 3-4) |
| The Void "between worlds" / "its three dimensions" | Superseded: the Void is the **axis of separation** along the cord, orthogonal to the places (Section 5) |

The sub-pages once planned here (`ordinary-world.md`, `safe-harbour.md`, `other-side.md`) are
cancelled; deepening happens against this topology when the worlds are specified further (DS-1
World Model derives from this core).
