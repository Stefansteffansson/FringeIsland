# Kickstarter — The Founding Moment

**Purpose:** The creative vision for FringeIsland's public launch — the Kickstarter campaign as Season Zero.

---

## Overview

The Kickstarter is not primarily a fundraiser. It is a **founding moment** — a community-building event, a world-launch, and an invitation. The campaign is Season Zero: the first chapter of FringeIsland's story told to the world.

When someone backs the campaign, they do not become a backer. They **arrive on the island.**

This directory holds the creative vision for that moment. Operational details (budget, timeline, video production) will live in planning when the time comes. What lives here is the *why* and the *what* — the story the campaign tells and the world it opens.
