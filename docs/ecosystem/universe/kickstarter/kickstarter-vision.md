# Kickstarter — Season Zero

**Status:** Extracted from founding vision (April 2026)
**Source:** OLD_VISION.md Part Six

---

## What the Kickstarter is

The first public chapter of FringeIsland's story will be told through a Kickstarter campaign.

Not primarily as a fundraiser. As a **founding moment** — a community-building event, a world-launch, and an invitation. The campaign is Season Zero.

---

## The creative vision

When someone backs the campaign, they do not become a backer. They **arrive on the island.** The rewards are arrival rituals, not merchandise. The campaign updates are the first episodes of the world. The campaign page is the first world-building document.

The campaign speaks to two audiences at once:

- **Founding Members** — who feel the pull of the three questions and want a world built around them
- **Founding Dreamineers** — who recognise the invitation to build a world worth living in

---

## The founding Dreamineer cohort

The founding Dreamineer cohort — the people who self-select by backing at the world-builder tier — is the most valuable thing the campaign will produce. More valuable than the funding. More valuable than the member count.

They are the first Dreamineers — the seed of the world's authorial community. The first creative community. (Dreamineer is an authorial mode, not a governing body; enterprise stewardship sits with the Universeers, the FringeIsland Council, and DeusEx — see the [roles core](../roles/README.md).) The people who will shape what FringeIsland becomes before the wider world arrives.

---

## Timing

The campaign launches after the full ecosystem vision is set. Not before.

Its design is its own exploration — a dedicated session, approached with the same care as everything else. The campaign is not a marketing exercise bolted onto the platform. It is the platform's first story, told to the world.
