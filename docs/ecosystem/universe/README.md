# The FringeIsland Universe

**Purpose:** The creative, conceptual, evolving description of FringeIsland as a world. What the universe is, how it works, and what it means to inhabit it.

This is the puzzle — pieces placed as they are defined, gaps visible where they are not. The full picture will continue to emerge as the ecosystem, the community, and the Dreamineer movement grow.

**This is for:** Universe design — cosmology, developmental mechanics, narrative structure, beings, community dynamics. The substance of what makes FringeIsland a world, not just a platform.

**This is NOT for:** Platform architecture or service specifications (→ `../../platform/`), product features (→ `../../products/`), open questions still being explored (→ `../thinking/`), or strategic/business documents (→ `../strategy/`).

**Relationship to other docs:**
- `../VISION.md` constrains — it says what FringeIsland is and isn't.
- `../MANIFESTO.md` inspires — it says what we value.
- This directory **imagines** — it says how the world actually works.
- `../../platform/domain/world-model/` will eventually implement what is described here.

**Graduation path:** Content starts in `../thinking/` as exploration. When it crystallises into a position about how the universe works, it moves here.
