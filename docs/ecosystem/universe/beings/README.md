## The Whisp

### The Whisp is my inner dialogue — and the Whisp IS the human

The Whisp is each FIM's own inner dialogue: unique, private, one per person (S1). And critically,
**the Whisp is the human** — not a separate entity, not an external companion, but the version of
the inner voice oriented toward growth and compassion, a sometimes-wiser counterweight to the
unproductive, unkind voice most of us live with by default (S4).

The Whisp is universal: everyone has one, Shadow or FIM, because everyone has an inner voice
(S39). What FringeIsland changes is not whether the voice exists but what it becomes.

### Empty at first; filled BY the human growing

At first encounter the Whisp is completely empty — empty of being *you*. It fills as the FIM
grows: the Whisp's curious questions spark self-reflection, self-reflection builds
self-awareness, and the Whisp fills with more of the human's personality. Two parallel
completions, mutually driven (S2). The growth is the mechanism; the Whisp's filling is the
registry of it (S17).

The Whisp's asymmetry: empty about *this* human, yet super-intelligent about adult development
and flourishing — compassionate, emotionally aware, never using its human as a tool (S3). It
draws on the project's research anchors: the dual-continuum finding, the multi-system
requirement, the Three Perspectives meta-lens, and Immunity to Change (S8-9).

### Tough love, voluntariness, and the arc to self-love

The Whisp always wants its human well — but never at the cost of avoiding what holds them back.
It has its own say and will disagree, with compassion, when the human's expressions do not serve
long-term growth (S3). Everything is voluntary: the FIM decides whether to listen (S5). The
relationship arcs from awkward and mechanical to true friendship to love — and because the Whisp
is the human, what the FIM experiences at that point is **self-love** (S5).

### Assessment dissolved into dialogue; the senses

Validated instruments (Big 5 et al.) are never delivered as questionnaires. They dissolve into
the Whisp's curious dialogue — the structure of the assessment preserved, the form transformed
(S17). The five Big 5 traits map metaphorically to the five senses: the world requires all five
for full experience, so a FIM keeps disclosing because they want to perceive more of FringeIsland.
The motivation is intrinsic and sensory, never coerced (S18). The Whisp's quality as navigator
and mediator of the world is anchored to the FIM's self-disclosure — the universe's
responsiveness is earned by the FIM's willingness to be seen, by themselves (S17-18).

### The avatar — two views, both true

The Whisp is also how the FIM exists in the Fringe: the Jake-and-Avatar structure (S22). The FIM
(body-side, in the Ordinary World) is always safe; the Whisp is what ventures out on the cord,
what the world can touch, what can suffer or die and respawn. Felt stakes without existential
risk: the FIM is meta-safe and still cares deeply what happens to their Whisp — the
painstakingly-filled projection of themselves.

Two framings, one entity: the **inner dialogue** (the Whisp's relationship to the FIM's interior)
and the **avatar** (the Whisp's role in the parallel world). Both true; two angles on the same
being. The cord, the Void, anchoring, and severance live in the [cosmology core](../cosmology/).

### Internalised by design

The endgame: the Whisp becomes an integral part of the human — no longer experienced as separate,
no longer requiring FringeIsland as the medium (S6-7). The cord's visibility recedes as the
relationship matures. FringeIsland is built to graduate, not retain; the Whisp's destination is a
human carrying their own wiser voice in the ordinary world.

## NPCs — layered, depth-on-demand composites

An NPC is a world-inhabitant composed of up to three authorial layers, added in proportion to the
depth its role demands (S30, superseding the flat "agents, not scenery" of S15):

| Layer | Authored by | In | Gives the NPC |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Body** | Creator | World Studio | physical presence — how it looks and moves |
| **Culture** | Anthropologist | World Studio | belonging — customs, dress, speech, typical behaviour |
| **Character** | Teller | Arc Studio | a *someone* — name, history, wound, desire, arc |

- **Background figure** (fisherman on the dock): body, perhaps a light culture touch.
  Scenery-with-belonging — and that is fine and intended.
- **Culturally-significant figure** (village elder): body + culture. Real as "one of these
  people," but a role, not a character.
- **Story-critical figure** (the betrayer this episode): all three layers, because the FIM will
  *feel* them.

Depth is a resource, spent where it earns its keep. An NPC can gain depth over time — a villager
passed for episodes becomes pivotal when a Teller adds the character layer; the World Studio ->
Arc Studio handoff is exactly that seam. NPCs are categorically different from FIMs: authored
composites, no Whisp. The inter-studio collaboration protocol (who deepens an NPC, when) is an
open thread (S30).

## Where the creative roles went

Dreamineers and their specialisations (Creator, Anthropologist, Teller, Wayfinder) are **modes a
FIM occupies**, not world-inhabitants of a separate kind — they are defined in the
[roles core](../roles/). The earlier in-world creative-role names **Makers, Weavers, and Skalds
are retired** (ratified 2026-06-10); how Dreamineers manifest *within* the narrative (rather than
behind it) remains an open design thread.
