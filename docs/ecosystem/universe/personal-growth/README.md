# Personal Growth — How Growth Works

**Purpose:** The personal growth mechanics of FringeIsland — what growth means, how it happens, and the structural concepts that make it possible.

---

## Overview

FringeIsland is built around three questions: *Who am I? What do I want? How do I get there?* These are not answered by the platform — they are held by it.

Growth happens through lived experience, not instruction. The developmental scaffolding is invisible in the experience layer — stories first, themes underneath.

This directory documents the structural concepts that underpin that scaffolding.
