# The Engagement Spectrum

**Status:** Extracted from founding vision (April 2026)
**Source:** OLD_VISION.md Part Two

---

## Overview

Members engage with FringeIsland along a natural spectrum. No one position is more valid than another. Every path leads to growth. Every position on the spectrum is welcome.

---

## The Spectrum

**Homebody** — tends their inner garden, builds their digital home, reflects quietly, and never ventures far into the wider narrative. This is a genuinely valuable way to inhabit FringeIsland. The Homebody can do deep individual work — the individual perspective (1) — through reflection, journaling, and solo journeys.

**Explorer** — deep in narrative arcs, seasonal episodes, quests, group dynamics, community contribution, world-building. Living in FringeIsland as a genuine second world. Explorers naturally engage the relational and communal perspectives (1+1, 1+community) — the ones that require other people.

**Beyond** — those who press further still, past the village and far out on the cord into the Fringe's deep reaches — the growth-bearing Void distance the world holds but does not yet fully reveal (see the [cosmology core](../cosmology/README.md)).

Between these: infinite gradations. Members move along the spectrum freely. There is no progression requirement — a Homebody is not "behind" an Explorer.

---

## The developmental honesty

The relational (1+1) and communal (1+community) perspectives cannot be collapsed alone. A member who never engages with others can still grow — but the full developmental arc of FringeIsland opens only through connection.

This is not a judgment of the Homebody path. It is the nature of the model.

The individual perspective can be explored through solo work — reflection, journaling, solo journeys. The relational and communal perspectives structurally require genuine human connection. Not NPC interactions. Not AI-generated activity. Other people.

The island invites, but does not force.

---

## Implications

- The platform must support the full spectrum — solo tools are as important as social features
- Homebody-oriented features (garden, journal, personal reflection) are not lesser or preliminary — they are a complete mode of engagement
- Community and group features are not add-ons — they are the only path to collapsing the relational and communal perspectives
- The cold-start problem (see [Open Questions CQ-001](../../thinking/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)) is particularly acute here: relational and communal perspectives need real people
