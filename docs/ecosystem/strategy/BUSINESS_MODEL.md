# Business Model

**Status:** Extracted from founding vision (April 2026)
**Source:** OLD_VISION.md Part Six

---

## Principle

FringeIsland sustains itself through revenue streams chosen because each aligns with the values rather than compromising them. The model is designed to protect independence in perpetuity.

---

## Five Revenue Streams

### 1. Member subscriptions and tiers

The backbone. FringeIsland is free to join — genuinely free, not a crippled demo. The free tier is a real home. Premium tiers unlock deeper experience: richer journeys, fuller seasonal participation, expanded creative tools.

The premium feels like going deeper, not like accessing what was already yours.

### 2. Voluntary donations

Members who want to actively support the Foundation's mission can. One-time or recurring. Two expressions:

- **Foundation support** — direct contribution to the Foundation's mission and operations
- **Dreamineer support** — direct support for individual Dreamineers whose work you love

Donations are a signal of ownership — of the feeling that this is your world too.

### 3. Marketplace revenue share

As the Dreamineer community grows and creates, a marketplace emerges. Premium journeys, skins, avatar items, physical artefacts, plugins, facilitator tools. The Foundation takes a fair platform percentage. Creators earn. The marketplace grows with the community — a flywheel that benefits everyone.

### 4. Events

Online seasonal events, regional community gatherings, and ultimately an annual FringeIsland Summit — where the community assembles, the world is celebrated, and the next season begins.

Events serve multiple functions simultaneously: revenue, belonging, visibility, and the discovery of new Dreamineers.

### 5. Endowment

The long-horizon ambition. A permanent investment fund whose returns sustain the Foundation in perpetuity — protecting FringeIsland from the financial instability that threatens even the most beloved open communities.

This is a **Beyond-Urd goal** — built toward from the beginning by directing surplus to the reserve rather than distributing it.

---

## Corporate independence

FringeIsland will never take venture capital. It will not offer equity to investors. It will not become beholden to corporate sponsors whose interests may one day diverge from the community's.

This is not naivety about money. It is clarity about what kind of organisation FringeIsland is. A movement for personal sovereignty cannot be owned by the forces that most threaten it.

Corporate sponsorship is deliberately excluded. The temptation will come as FringeIsland grows and its brand becomes valuable. The line is held here, in the founding document, before that temptation arrives.
