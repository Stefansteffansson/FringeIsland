# Experience Principles

## Overview

These principles describe how FringeIsland should *feel* when experienced — across all products (Hub, Gimbal, Game) and all touchpoints. They bridge universe design (what the world is) and product implementation (how the world is rendered as software). They are design constraints, not feature specs.

---

## 1. Three Worlds, Felt Transitions

The UI reflects which world the member inhabits: Ordinary World, Safe Harbour, The Other Side. Transitions between worlds are *felt* — through colour, typography, and ambient elements — not just navigated. Each world has its own aesthetic language and emotional register.

The interface itself is a narrative device.

---

## 2. The Whisp as Constant Companion

The Whisp is always present — ambient when quiet, active during encounters. Its fidelity grows visibly with member engagement. Encounters feel personal, warm, and non-prescriptive.

The Whisp is a persistent UI element, not a feature you visit.

---

## 3. Stories First, Themes Underneath

Learning emerges implicitly from narrative, never as lectures. Seasons and episodes provide temporal rhythm. A-plot, B-stories, and sub-plots create layered engagement.

A member could engage purely for the story and still have a valuable experience. The developmental scaffolding is always there for those who look deeper — but it is never imposed.

This is the non-judgment principle expressed as a design rule.

---

## 4. Creator-Friendly by Default

Journey Studio is the central creative tool — intuitive for Makers, powerful for Weavers. The publishing workflow supports quality without gatekeeping. Revenue sharing is transparent and fair.

Creation is a first-class experience, not an admin tool.
