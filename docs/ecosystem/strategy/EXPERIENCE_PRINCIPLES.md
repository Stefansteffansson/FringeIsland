## Overview

These principles describe how FringeIsland should *feel* when experienced — across both equipment profiles (The Hub, the canvas surface; The Gimbal, the senses surface) and all touchpoints. They bridge universe design (what the world is) and product implementation (how the world is rendered as software). They are design constraints, not feature specs.

---

## 1. The Worlds, Felt Transitions

The UI reflects where the member is: the Ordinary World, the near side seen through the Shimmer, the village and private home in the Beyond, the deep reaches of the hostile place. Transitions are *felt* — through colour, typography, and ambient elements — not just navigated. The warm place and the hostile place are two poles of one continuum, and the interface carries that tonal range; crossing the Shimmer, stepping through the ball, or venturing out on the cord each has its own emotional register.

The interface itself is a narrative device.

## 2. The Whisp as Constant Companion

The Whisp is always present — ambient when quiet, active during encounters. It fills *by* the member growing, and that filling is felt: perception through the Shimmer sharpens, the world becomes more available. The cord carries the living signals (health as colour and integrity; salience receding as the Whisp internalises) — never rankings. Encounters feel personal, warm, and non-prescriptive.

The Whisp is a persistent UI element, not a feature you visit.

---

## 3. Stories First, Themes Underneath

Learning emerges implicitly from narrative, never as lectures. Seasons and episodes provide temporal rhythm. A-plot, B-stories, and sub-plots create layered engagement.

A member could engage purely for the story and still have a valuable experience. The developmental scaffolding is always there for those who look deeper — but it is never imposed.

This is the non-judgment principle expressed as a design rule.

---

## 4. Creator-Friendly by Default

The studios — World, Arc, and Journey under Universe Studio — are a role-gated authoring mode inside the experience, not an admin tool. Journey Studio is intuitive for a first-time Wayfinder and powerful for a seasoned one; every FIM gets a first taste of authoring through the personal-scope slice of World Studio (furnishing their own home). The publishing workflow supports quality without gatekeeping. Revenue sharing is transparent and fair.

Creation is a first-class experience.
