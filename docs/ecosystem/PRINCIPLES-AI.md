# AI Principles — how FringeIsland creates with AI

**Status:** Constitutional. Graduated from the universe-discovery work (Statement 24, with
Statement 13) in reconciliation Session B, ratified 2026-06-10.
**Referenced from:** `MANIFESTO.md` and `VISION.md`. Where any practice conflicts with this
document, this document wins.

---

## The principle

> AI is an extension of human capability — it speeds production and widens the space of
> perspectives beyond what any individual could hold, while authorship, integrity, privacy, and
> the originating creative intention remain wholly human. The human's first thought opens the
> work; AI expands the field; the human's last say closes it. Guard rails — human-authored and
> bidirectional — keep AI in its lane and help the human stay on the road they themselves chose.

FringeIsland is AI-first AND human-first: AI is used heavily in every way that benefits the
universe, but privacy is never sacrificed for AI, individual contributions are never sacrificed
for AI, and humans always come first when they conflict (Statement 13).

## AI is an extension, not an autonomous worker

There is no autonomy dial to ration, because authorship was never delegated. AI serves two
functions:

- **Speed** — collapsing production cost: sites, in-world documents, NPC voices, images, audio,
  micro-apps in hours, not months.
- **Breadth of perspective** — surfacing framings, ideas, and references from a span no individual
  could hold; not to decide, but to widen the choice set the human is choosing from.

## The last say is authorship; the first thought is sacred

The creative act *is* the choosing, shaping, and rejecting. Everything before the last say is raw
material; the last say is the work. And the subtler risk is not AI overriding the human but AI
*anchoring* the human — seven good-enough options shown early, so the human's own harder-won
eighth is never born. Therefore the sequence is fixed:

1. **Human intent first** (Stage 1 — human-first)
2. **AI expansion second** (Stage 2 — speed and breadth, within the rails)
3. **Human selection third** (Stage 3 — the last say)

## Dreaming sessions

Stage 1 is protected by a named ritual: **dreaming sessions** — facilitated ideation and the early
steps of design thinking, run human-first, *before* AI expands the field. AI may be present as a
**facilitator** (asking, prompting, holding structure) but never as a **generator** (proposing
content) in this phase. Dreamineers run dreaming sessions.

## Guard railing — bidirectional, always human-authored

The human is behind the wheel; guard railing is the safety system around that, and it points in
two directions:

- **Rails that constrain AI** (keep AI in its lane): AI does not act without human sign-off, does
  not touch privacy, does not ship unreviewed work, does not pre-empt the human's originating
  intention.
- **Rails that protect the human driver** (catch a drift before it becomes harm): AI flags the
  human's own blind spots against the human's own stated values — "this scene may land as
  genuinely frightening rather than playfully ambiguous"; "this real-world location raises a
  safety concern"; "six dark episodes in a row — intentional?"

FringeIsland specifically needs both: the universe deliberately includes a hostile pole, blurs
reality and fiction, and pushes FIMs toward the growth zone. Each is a curve in the road where a
well-meaning author could drift from productive challenge into actual harm.

**The rails are always human-authored.** If AI defined its own guard rails, authorship of the
constraints would have been handed to the thing being constrained. Humans decide the rails; AI
operates within them and helps enforce them — including back upon the humans.
