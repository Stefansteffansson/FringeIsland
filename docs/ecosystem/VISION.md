> *En värld med friska vindar och varma ljuspunkter.*
> *A world of fresh winds and warm points of light.*

> *To equip people, groups and communities with the best tools and capabilities to live, grow and matter — in a balanced and harmonious way.*

---

## Purpose

FringeIsland is an ecosystem built around three questions:

- **Who am I?**
- **What do I want?**
- **How do I get there?**

These questions are not answered by the platform. They are held by it — given space, structure, and companionship — so that each member can find their own answers.

Beneath the questions lie the drives: all humans want **to live** (engaged in the moment, not postponing happiness), **to grow** (out of curiosity and delight — never because they are not good enough), and **to matter** (to themselves, and through contribution to others). The questions are how a person investigates; the drives are why the investigation matters. The three dance in balance — none is ever complete, and each feeds the other two.

FringeIsland is **built to graduate, not retain**. Over time the experience internalises: the member carries their own wiser inner voice into ordinary life and no longer needs the platform. That is success.

---

## What this is

An immersive edutainment platform where personal development happens through lived experience, not instruction. Members grow by entering a world, not by completing a course. The Hero's Journey is the narrative framework. Entertainment is the surface. Development is the consequence.

## Structural concepts

**FIM** (FringeIsland Member) — the base identity: a person with a Whisp. Entrants begin as **Shadows** (anonymous, ephemeral, near-side only) and may **transcend** into FIMs — the consent-and-persistence threshold, which grants their glowing glass ball in the Tree.

**The worlds** — the cosmological frame: the *Ordinary World* (daily life — always present, never absent), the *Shimmer* (the membrane between), and the *Fringe* — two co-located places behind the one Shimmer: a warm, welcoming place and a hostile, cold one, two poles of a single continuum, each with a near side (tied to worldly coordinates) and a Beyond. The *village* — the safe-harbour commons with the Tree and its glowing glass balls — lies in the Beyond of the warm place. The *Void* is the axis of separation: the medium the Whisp hangs in, out on its cord.

→ *See [Universe / cosmology](./universe/cosmology/) — the canonical core.*

**The Whisp** — each FIM's own inner dialogue. The Whisp IS the FIM: a sometimes-wiser counterweight to the default inner voice, empty at first and filled *by* the FIM's growing self-awareness. It is also the avatar through which the FIM walks the Fringe — perceiving through the Shimmer, exposed on the cord while the human stays safe. Its destination is internalisation: a human carrying their own wiser voice, without the platform.

**Three Perspectives** — Individual (1), Relationship (1+1), Collective (1+Community). The relationship and collective perspectives require others to collapse, creating developmental interdependence by design.

**Dreamineers** — FIMs in their authorial mode: **Creators** and **Anthropologists** (World Studio), **Tellers** (Arc Studio), **Wayfinders** (Journey Studio) — authoring the world, its stories, and its journeys. Roles are modes a person occupies, not kinds of people.

→ *See [Universe / roles](./universe/roles/) — the canonical core.*

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Non-judgment** — FringeIsland takes no position on questions of meaning or continuity. It guides members toward their own answers.
2. **Stories as entertainment first** — developmental themes are invisible in the foundation layer. Learning emerges implicitly from experience.
3. **Developmental interdependence** — growth is structurally social. The Three Perspectives make collective participation necessary, not optional.
4. **Privacy over commercial opportunity** — member data serves the member, not the platform.
5. **Direction over destination** — the ecosystem orients. It does not prescribe.
6. **Growth is delight, not deficiency** — FringeIsland never runs on "you are not good enough." Guard this.
7. **Built to graduate** — the platform succeeds when members outgrow it.
8. **Humans first in an AI-first practice** — AI extends human capability; authorship, integrity, privacy, and the first thought remain human. → *See [AI Principles](./PRINCIPLES-AI.md).*

→ *See [Manifesto](./MANIFESTO.md) for the full set of cultural values.*

## Ecosystem boundaries

FringeIsland is **not**:

- A course platform with certificates
- A social network optimised for engagement
- A therapy service or clinical tool
- A gamified productivity app

---

## Ecosystem composition

The ecosystem consists of **products** — the shipped surfaces of one experience, defined by equipment profiles, not devices (*The Hub*, the canvas surface; *The Gimbal*, the senses surface; the Game is a depth setting of journeys, not a product) — **platform services** (shared infrastructure and domain logic), **studios** (the role-gated authoring mode: World, Arc, and Journey Studios under Universe Studio as their binding frame), and a **design system** (shared visual language).

→ *See [Products](../products/), [Platform](../platform/), [Studios](../studios/) for entity descriptions, and ADR-U025/U026 for the entity decisions.*
