# FringeIsland — Vision

**Status:** Constitutional document
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Purpose

FringeIsland is an ecosystem built around three questions:

- **Who am I?**
- **What do I want?**
- **How do I get there?**

These questions are not answered by the platform. They are held by it — given space, structure, and companionship — so that each member can find their own answers.

---

## What this is

An immersive edutainment platform where personal development happens through lived experience, not instruction. Members grow by entering a world, not by completing a course. The Hero's Journey is the narrative framework. Entertainment is the surface. Development is the consequence.

---

## Structural concepts

**FIM** (FringeIsland Member) — anyone who enters the ecosystem.

**Three Worlds** — the cosmological frame:

- *The Ordinary World* — life outside the platform. Always present, never absent.
- *The Safe Harbour* — FringeIsland itself. A place of reflection, sharing, and becoming. Safe, not comfortable.
- *The Other Side* — deeper water. Where narrative, challenge, and transformation live. Seasons and episodes unfold here.

→ *See [World Model](../platform/domain/world-model/) for creative depth on each world.*

**The Whisp** — each FIM's personal future self. Not an answer-giver but a perceptual lens, operating across all three worlds as companion voice, perceptual richness, and active instrument.

**Three Dimensions** — Individual (1), Relationship (1+1), Collective (1+Community). The relationship and collective dimensions require others to collapse, creating developmental interdependence by design.

**Dreamineers / Skalds** — narrative creators and facilitators who author the world's seasons, journeys, and experiences.

---

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Non-judgment** — FringeIsland takes no position on questions of meaning or continuity. It guides members toward their own answers.
2. **Stories as entertainment first** — developmental themes are invisible in the foundation layer. Learning emerges implicitly from experience.
3. **Developmental interdependence** — growth is structurally social. The Three Dimensions make collective participation necessary, not optional.
4. **Privacy over commercial opportunity** — member data serves the member, not the platform.
5. **Direction over destination** — the ecosystem orients. It does not prescribe.

→ *See [Manifesto](./MANIFESTO.md) for the full set of cultural values.*

---

## Ecosystem boundaries

FringeIsland is **not**:

- A course platform with certificates
- A social network optimised for engagement
- A therapy service or clinical tool
- A gamified productivity app

---

## Ecosystem composition

The ecosystem consists of **products** (surfaces FIMs touch), **platform services** (shared infrastructure and domain logic), **studios** (authoring tools for Dreamineers), and a **design system** (shared visual language).
