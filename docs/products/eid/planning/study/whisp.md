# The Whisp

**Wave:** Eid (concept & definition) — deeper integration in Heim and beyond
**Category:** Features
**Status:** 🔴 Needs concept work

---

## What Is This

The Whisp is the FringeIsland member's inner dialogue partner.
It always follows the individual member on their journeys and endeavors.
The Whisp is a deeply personal presence — not a chatbot, not a tool,
but a companion that knows the member and accompanies them through their growth.

---

## Why We Are Building This

The Whisp is what makes FringeIsland experientially distinct.
It transforms the platform from a collection of features into a living,
personalised journey. Without the Whisp, FringeIsland is a platform.
With the Whisp, it becomes an experience.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

*(To be developed in depth during the concept and study phases.
The following are initial directional thoughts, not specifications.)*

- The Whisp is unique to each member
- It accompanies the member across all contexts — real world, the Void, FringeIsland
- It has a voice, a presence, a way of engaging that feels personal
- It deepens over time as it learns the member's patterns, reflections and growth
- In Eid its implementation is a concept outline and foundational definition —
  deeper integration comes in Heim (Journey Studio v.2) and Brim (across all three dimensions)
