## What Is This

The first full public release of the FringeIsland iOS mobile app — available
to all FringeIsland members. This is the graduation from beta (v.01 in Brim)
to a production-quality release.

## Why We Are Building This

Mobile is the primary daily touchpoint for most members. v.1 is the moment
FringeIsland becomes fully mobile-native and accessible everywhere.
