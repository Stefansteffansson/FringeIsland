# Direct Messaging

**Wave:** Ferd
**Category:** Communication
**Status:** 🟡 Needs study

---

## What Is This

Direct messaging enables members to communicate privately — one-to-one
or in small groups — outside the context of a public group forum.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Private communication between members is a basic social need on any platform.
It enables collaboration, mentorship and connection that doesn't belong in a public forum.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

- A member can send a direct message to another individual member
- A member can send a message to multiple members simultaneously (group DM)
- Messages are private to the participants
