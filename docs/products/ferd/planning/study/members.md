# Members

**Wave:** Ferd
**Category:** General
**Status:** 🟡 Needs study

---

## What Is This

A Member is an authenticated individual on the FringeIsland platform.
Members are the primary actors — everything on the platform exists in relation
to members and their journeys, connections and growth.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Members are the foundation of the platform. Without a well-defined member model
nothing else — groups, journeys, permissions, communication — can be built correctly.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

- A member has an identity on the platform (profile, display name, etc.)
- A member belongs to one or more groups
- A member has a personal group created automatically on sign-up
- A member's permissions are derived exclusively through their group memberships
- A member has a travel journal for personal notes
