# Forum

**Wave:** Ferd
**Category:** Communication
**Status:** 🟡 Needs study

---

## What Is This

Each group has a forum — a shared space for members of that group to communicate,
discuss and share in a structured, threaded format.

---

## Why We Are Building This

The forum is the primary shared communication space within a group.
It enables community, discussion and the building of shared knowledge within a group context.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

- Each group has its own forum
- Only members of a group can access that group's forum
- The forum is threaded — discussions are organised into topics and replies
- Permissions for posting, moderating and managing the forum are role-based
