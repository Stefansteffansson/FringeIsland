# Journals

**Wave:** Ferd
**Category:** General
**Status:** 🟡 Needs study

---

## What Is This

The Journal is a personal space for each member to take notes during their
journeys and experiences on FringeIsland. It is private by default and
belongs exclusively to the individual member.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Reflection is a core part of the FringeIsland growth philosophy. The journal
gives members a place to record thoughts, insights and observations as they
progress through their journeys.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

- Every member has a journal
- Journal entries are private to the member by default
- Entries can be associated with a journey, an episode, or be free-form
- The journal persists across journeys
