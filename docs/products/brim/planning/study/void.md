# The Void

**Wave:** Brim
**Category:** Features
**Status:** 🔴 Needs concept work

---

## What Is This

The Void is the liminal dimension between the real world and FringeIsland.
It is neither fully grounded in reality nor fully within the island's world —
it is the in-between space, the passage, the threshold.

---

## Why We Are Building This

The three-dimensional universe (real world / Void / FringeIsland) is what makes
the FringeIsland experience genuinely immersive and distinct. The Void is the
connective tissue — without it, the two worlds are isolated from each other.
With it, the journey becomes a continuous, dimensionally rich experience.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

*(To be fully developed in concept phase. Directional notes only.)*

- The Void exists between the real world and FringeIsland
- The Whisp functions in all three dimensions but may behave differently in the Void
- Members pass through the Void when transitioning between worlds
- In Brim the Void is introduced and Dreamineers can begin developing it
  — but only for testing and experimental purposes
- Full Void development continues beyond Brim
