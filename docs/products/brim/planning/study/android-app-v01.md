## What Is This

The first beta release of the FringeIsland Android mobile app — delivered to a limited
number of members with limited functionality. Parallel to the iOS beta and subject
to the same scope and constraints.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Ensuring Android parity from the start means FringeIsland is accessible to the
full range of mobile users and avoids the trap of iOS-first thinking that
creates costly Android catch-up later.
