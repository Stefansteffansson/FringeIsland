# My Garden

**Wave:** Heim
**Category:** Features
**Status:** 🔴 Needs concept work

---

## What Is This

My Garden is each FringeIsland member's unique personal home on the island.
It is their own space within the FringeIsland world — a place that belongs
to them, reflects them, and grows with them.

---

## Why We Are Building This

A personal home on the island makes FringeIsland feel inhabited and personal
rather than generic. It gives members a stake in the world — something that
is uniquely theirs — which deepens engagement and belonging.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

*(To be fully developed in concept phase. Directional notes only.)*

- Every member has a Garden created automatically when they first arrive on FringeIsland
- The Garden is personal — it belongs to and reflects the individual member
- In Heim, members can view their Garden but customisation is limited
  (deeper member customisation comes in Brim via FringeIsland Studio v.2)
- The Garden may reflect journey progress, achievements or personal milestones
- The Whisp has a presence in the Garden
