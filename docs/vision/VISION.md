## Part One — The Why

### The Problem Worth Solving

Most people move through life carrying three questions they can't quite answer.

*Who am I?*
*What do I want?*
*How do I get there?*

Not once. Not just in adolescence. These questions return at every threshold — when school ends, when relationships change, when careers shift, when the self we thought we were turns out to be only a draft. They return at 22 and at 45 and at 67. They are not problems to solve once and move past. They are the living questions of a human life.

And yet most people face them alone, or not at all.

The tools that exist for self-discovery are either clinical and cold (personality tests that spit out a type and move on), shallow and performative (social media as self-presentation theatre), or inaccessible — reserved for those with money, time, or the right therapist. The spaces that exist for community tend toward performance over presence. Most environments reward arriving with a polished identity rather than honestly exploring an incomplete one.

We live in a world saturated with content and starved of genuine experience. With information and starved of reflection. With connection and starved of belonging.

FringeIsland is a response to that gap.

### The Vision

We are building an alternative world — not as escapism, but as a practice space for the real one.

A world where it is safe to try things out. Safe to fail. Safe to not know who you are yet. A world with its own logic, its own landscape, its own cast of characters and ongoing story — but rooted always in the three questions that matter in every world.

We call it FringeIsland. It exists in the fringe of ordinary life — close enough to feel real, different enough to feel free.

The SFM vision, from which FringeIsland grew, says it simply:

*En värld med friska vindar och varma ljuspunkter.*
*A world of fresh winds and warm points of light.*

That is the feeling. That is what we are building toward.

## Part Two — What FringeIsland Is

### Not a Product — A Movement

FringeIsland is not an app. It is not a platform. It is not a game.

It is a movement — with an ecosystem.

The web platform is one node in that ecosystem. The native apps will be another. The physical gatherings, the 3D-printed artefacts of members' digital homes, the AR layer blending the island into the physical world, the marketplace of Dreamineer-created experiences, the annual Summit where the community assembles in real life — all of these are nodes. The movement is the whole.

What holds them together is not a brand or a technology stack. It is a shared world, a shared set of questions, and a shared commitment to the kind of growth that only happens when you feel genuinely safe.

### Immersive Edutainment

FringeIsland lives at the intersection of three things that are usually kept apart:

**Immersive** — an alternative reality world with its own geography, narrative, characters, seasons and rules. A place you can actually inhabit, not just visit. Inspired by the worlds of The Matrix, Inception, Westworld, Limitless, Under the Dome — stories that create a reality alongside reality and use it to reveal something true.

**Educational** — grounded in research-based frameworks for self-understanding. Not lectures. Not courses. Tools woven into the fabric of the experience: the Big 5/OCEAN personality model, VIA Character Strengths, Erin Meyer's Culture Map, and others. The island teaches without feeling like teaching.

**Entertainment** — experienced as story. Seasons and episodes. An A-plot, B-stories, sub-plots. The world has a mythology, a mystery, an unfolding. Members don't just use FringeIsland — they experience it. And through that experience, something real changes.

The word we use for this combination is **Immersive Edutainment**. It is not a compromise between the three — it is the synthesis.

### The World

FringeIsland exists across three realms:

**Earth** — the ordinary world. Where members live. The departure point.

**Fringe Island** — the alternative world. The island itself. Where the story unfolds, where the journey happens, where the self is explored and gradually revealed. It has its own landscape, its own communities, its own lore.

**The Other Side** — the deeper mystery. What lies beyond the island's edges. A space that narrative reaches toward but does not yet fully reveal.

The story runs in **Seasons and Episodes** — S1:E1, S1:E2, and so on. Each season has an arc. Each episode advances it. The Dreamineers create and weave the content. Members experience it, contribute to it, and through their engagement, shape its trajectory.

### The Red Thread — From Empty to Whole

Every member arrives on FringeIsland as an undefined soul.

Their avatar begins completely empty. No identity. No character. No story yet. That emptiness is not a limitation — it is the invitation. The whole point.

Through narrative, challenge, reflection and connection, the avatar gradually becomes a mirror of the member's true self. Not a curated self. Not a performed self. The actual self — values, strengths, blindspots, desires, fears, edges. The three instruments that shape this journey are:

**Who am I?** — Research-based tools for self-understanding. The Big 5/OCEAN model. VIA Character Strengths. Culture Map. Not tests that categorise you, but tools that illuminate you.

**What do I want?** — Purpose, values and vision discovery. What matters. What you're moving toward. The difference between what you've been told to want and what actually calls you.

**How do I get there?** — Skills, habits, relationships and pathways. The practical architecture of becoming.

This is the red thread. It runs through everything — every journey, every episode, every group, every conversation. You can engage with it lightly or deeply. You can move through it quickly or slowly. But it is always there: the invitation to know yourself more honestly.

### The Engagement Spectrum

Members engage with FringeIsland along a natural spectrum. No one position is more valid than another.

At one end: the **Homebody** — who tends their inner garden, builds their digital home, reflects quietly, and never ventures far into the wider narrative. This is a complete and valuable way to inhabit FringeIsland.

At the other: the **Explorer** — deep in narrative arcs, seasonal episodes, quests, group dynamics, community contribution, world-building. Living in FringeIsland as a genuine second world. And beyond even that: those who press further still, toward the edges of the island and whatever lies beyond — toward **The Other Side**, the deeper mystery that the world holds but does not yet fully reveal.

Between them: infinite gradations. Every path leads to growth. Every position on the spectrum is welcome.

## Part Three — Who FringeIsland Is For

### The Members

FringeIsland is built first for **young adults aged 18 to 29**.

This is not arbitrary. It is the threshold decade — when the identity inherited from childhood begins to feel like it doesn't fit, and the identity that will carry you through adult life has not yet solidified. When the three questions are loudest. When the stakes of getting it wrong feel highest, and when the capacity to change is still wide open.

FringeIsland meets people at that threshold with a world that takes the questions seriously.

Members aged **30 and beyond** are fully and genuinely welcome. The three questions do not expire. They return at every threshold, and FringeIsland is designed to receive people at any of them.

Members aged **50 and above** occupy a particularly important role — not just as experience consumers but as **contributors, mentors, lore-keepers and wisdom-sharers**. Life experience is a creative resource. The 50+ member who has already lived through multiple threshold moments brings something irreplaceable to the community and to the world-building. They are welcomed with intention, not as an afterthought.

**Under 18 is explicitly excluded.** FringeIsland cannot responsibly guarantee safe forum communication or age-appropriate experience content for minors. This is a values-driven decision — not a gatekeeping one. The world is built for adults who can choose it freely.

### The Dreamineers

The members experience FringeIsland. The **Dreamineers** build it.

Dreamineers are the creative community — the world-builders, storytellers, experience architects and makers who bring the island to life. They fall into two complementary roles:

**Makers** — content creators. They write the narratives, design the worlds, build the challenges, create the media, produce the episodes. They are the authors and artists of FringeIsland.

**Weavers** — experience architects. They take what Makers create and stitch it into coherent, meaningful end-to-end journeys. They ensure that the sum is greater than the parts. They are the curators and choreographers of the member experience.

Together, Makers and Weavers form a living creative organism. The island evolves because they tend it. The Dreamineer community is not a team of employees or a pool of contractors — it is a meritocratic, self-governing creative community whose best members naturally rise to stewardship of the world they love.

## Part Four — How FringeIsland Is Governed

### The Three-Layer Structure

FringeIsland is governed by a structure designed to protect its values in perpetuity — across leadership changes, commercial pressures, and the natural drift that affects any organisation over time.

**Layer 1 — The FringeIsland Foundation**

The Foundation is the guardian. Lean, permanent, and uncapturable.

It holds the core values, the red thread, the manifesto, the brand and the IP. It is established as a non-profit stichting — the Dutch legal form that Blender Foundation also uses — or an equivalent structure. It cannot be bought. It cannot be sold. It cannot be captured by any single interest, including its founder.

The Foundation does not run the day-to-day. It holds the line on what FringeIsland is, and what it will never become.

**Layer 2 — The Dreamineer Council**

The Council is the creative and quality steward. It governs narrative direction, season and episode approval, experience quality, and the standards by which the world is built.

Membership in the Council is not appointed — it is earned through contribution. The Dreamineers who show up consistently, who build the best things, who demonstrate care for the world and for the community — they become the Council. This is meritocracy in its truest sense: not a hierarchy of titles, but a recognition of genuine contribution.

**Layer 3 — The Open Community**

All members. All contributors. Plugin builders. Event organisers. Physical product makers. The people who show up and make FringeIsland richer by being here.

The Open Community operates freely within the values framework. The best rise naturally into the Council. The Council operates within the values framework set by the Foundation. Each layer is accountable to the one above it — and each layer is served by the one above it.

### Privacy and Data Sovereignty

This is not a policy. It is a founding principle — as non-negotiable as the governance structure itself.

Member data is owned by the member, and only the member. Always. Without exception.

Personal data is never shared with third parties. Never sold. Never leveraged for advertising or commercial partnerships of any kind.

Anonymised aggregate data may one day be explored — but only with explicit informed consent, responsible handling, and clear member benefit. Any decision touching personal data falls under the direct stewardship of the Foundation and the Dreamineer Council. It cannot be delegated.

This principle protects members. It also protects FringeIsland — because a community built on trust cannot afford to betray it once.

### On Corporate Independence

FringeIsland will never take venture capital. It will not offer equity to investors. It will not become beholden to corporate sponsors whose interests may one day diverge from the community's.

This is not naivety about money. It is clarity about what kind of organisation FringeIsland is. A movement for personal sovereignty cannot be owned by the forces that most threaten it.

Corporate sponsorship is deliberately excluded. The temptation will come as FringeIsland grows and its brand becomes valuable. The line is held here, in the founding document, before that temptation arrives.

## Part Five — The Creative and IP Framework

### Open the Tools, Protect the Soul

FringeIsland's creative ecosystem depends on openness — the freedom for anyone to contribute, build, remix and extend. But openness without protection leads to capture. The IP model is designed to enable maximum creative contribution while protecting the values and identity that make FringeIsland worth contributing to.

**The platform code** is fully open — MIT or Apache 2.0 licence. Auditable, forkable, contributable by anyone.

**Community experience content** — narratives, episodes, world-building, tools, frameworks — is shared under CC BY-SA with a Contributor Licence Agreement. Free to remix with attribution. Share-alike required. No non-commercial restriction: the marketplace is enabled from day one, and creators can earn from what they build. The CLA ensures the Foundation can steward this content in the marketplace while creators retain their copyright.

**Physical products, plugins, add-ons and extensions** operate under a commercial marketplace licence. Creators set prices. The Foundation takes a platform percentage. Creators keep the rest. This is the Blender Market model — generous to creators, sustainable for the Foundation.

The principle underlying all three layers: *open the tools, protect the soul.*

## Part Six — How FringeIsland Sustains Itself

### The Business Model

FringeIsland sustains itself through five revenue streams, chosen because each aligns with the values rather than compromising them.

**Member subscriptions and tiers.** The backbone. FringeIsland is free to join — genuinely free, not a crippled demo. The free tier is a real home. Premium tiers unlock deeper experience: richer journeys, fuller seasonal participation, expanded creative tools. The premium feels like going deeper, not like accessing what was already yours.

**Voluntary donations.** Members who want to actively support the Foundation's mission can. One-time or recurring. Two expressions: support for the Foundation itself, and direct support for Dreamineers whose work you love. Donations are a signal of ownership — of the feeling that this is your world too.

**Marketplace revenue share.** As the Dreamineer community grows and creates, a marketplace emerges. Premium journeys, skins, avatar items, physical artefacts, plugins, facilitator tools. The Foundation takes a fair platform percentage. Creators earn. The marketplace grows with the community — a flywheel that benefits everyone.

**Events.** Online seasonal events, regional community gatherings, and ultimately an annual FringeIsland Summit — where the community assembles, the world is celebrated, and the next season begins. Events serve multiple functions at once: revenue, belonging, visibility, and the discovery of new Dreamineers.

**Endowment.** The long-horizon ambition. A permanent investment fund whose returns sustain the Foundation in perpetuity — protecting FringeIsland from the financial instability that threatens even the most beloved open communities. This is a Phase 3 goal, built toward from the beginning by directing surplus to the reserve rather than distributing it.

### The Founding Moment — Kickstarter

The first public chapter of FringeIsland's story will be told through a Kickstarter campaign.

Not primarily as a fundraiser. As a **founding moment** — a community-building event, a world-launch, and an invitation. The campaign is Season Zero.

When someone backs the campaign, they do not become a backer. They **arrive on the island**. The rewards are arrival rituals, not merchandise. The campaign updates are the first episodes of the world. The campaign page is the first world-building document.

The campaign speaks to two audiences at once: founding Members who feel the pull of the three questions, and founding Dreamineers who recognise the invitation to build a world worth living in.

The founding Dreamineer cohort — the people who self-select by backing at the world-builder tier — is the most valuable thing the campaign will produce. More valuable than the funding. More valuable than the member count. They are the seed of the Dreamineer Council.

The campaign launches after the full ecosystem vision is set. Not before. Its design is its own exploration — a dedicated session, approached with the same care as everything else.

## Part Seven — The Platform and the Ecosystem

### The Platform Follows the Vision

The web platform currently in development is a proof of concept — a demonstration that the technical foundation is viable. It does not define or constrain what FringeIsland becomes.

The platform is built in phases, each phase validated before the next begins. The vision drives each phase — not the other way around.

**Phase 1 — Web Platform as Hub**
The web platform is the primary interface: identity, profiles, home gardens, groups, journey browsing, forums, contribution tools. Optimised as a Progressive Web App for mobile access. Discord serves as a temporary community scaffold — deliberately temporary. It will be retired when the platform is ready to hold the community itself.

**Phase 2 — Full PWA**
Push notifications for episode drops and narrative events. Basic AR experiments through camera access — location-based, QR-triggered world moments. Offline access to journey content. Discord retired. The community lives fully on FringeIsland's own platform. The marketplace launches.

**Phase 3 — Native Apps and Full AR**
Native iOS and Android apps built with validated knowledge of what the AR layer actually needs. The web platform remains — as the primary home for deep work, contribution, and desktop-first experiences. The native apps are the experiential layer: immersive, mobile, world-blending.

### The Broader Ecosystem

The platform is one node. The ecosystem extends further:

A game — or games — that bring the world to life in interactive form. Physical representations of FringeIsland worlds. 3D-printed artefacts: member homes, gardens, avatars, items — digital identity made physical. Physical meetups, training camps, retreats. An AR layer that places elements of the island in the real world. A marketplace of Dreamineer-created experiences, tools and objects. An annual Summit where the community meets in person and the next season is launched.

The ecosystem is not a product roadmap. It is a living organism — growing as the community grows, shaped by the Dreamineers who build it, held by the Foundation that protects it.

## Part Eight — What We Are Still Discovering

FringeIsland is not finished being imagined. Four areas require dedicated exploration before the full picture is complete:

**The Contribution Architecture** — How the platform opens itself to contribution. What can be built by anyone, what requires Dreamineer status, what is held only by the Foundation and Council. The rules of creative openness.

**The FringeIsland Manifesto** — The direction-setting principles of the movement. In the spirit of the Agile Manifesto: not rules, but orientations. X over Y. Showing direction, not fixing destination. Some anchors are already clear: member privacy over commercial opportunity. Personal growth over performance metrics. Open contribution over closed gatekeeping. Direction over rigid destination. Community ownership over corporate control. Safe experimentation over fear of failure. Lived experience over passive consumption.

**The First Experience — Season One, Episode One** — The founding narrative. The story that members first enter. The mystery, the conflict, the world that comes to life for the first time. This is the creative work that will define everything that follows.

**The Kickstarter Campaign** — The story, the tiers, the video, the funding goal, the pre-launch strategy. To be designed with the same care as the world itself.

A world. With fresh winds. And warm points of light.

A place where you arrive not knowing who you are, and leave — or rather, keep arriving — more honestly yourself.

That is what we are building.
