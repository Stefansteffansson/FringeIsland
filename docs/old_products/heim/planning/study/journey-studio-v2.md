# Journey Studio v.2

**Wave:** Heim
**Category:** Features
**Status:** 🔴 Needs concept work

---

## What Is This

The second version of Journey Studio expands journey authoring to include
FringeIsland as a setting alongside the real world. It also deepens the
integration of the Whisp and the travel Journal within journeys.

---

## Why We Are Building This

Once FringeIsland as a world exists (from earlier in Heim), journeys can
meaningfully span both real and imaginary dimensions. Dreamineers need the
tools to author those richer, multi-dimensional experiences.

---

## How It Is Supposed to Work

*(To be developed in study phase once FringeIsland world is defined.)*

- All capabilities of Journey Studio v.1 are retained and refined
- Dreamineers can now design journey stages that take place on FringeIsland
- The Whisp can be woven into journey design — prompts, reactions, guidance
- Journal integration is deeper — journeys can include structured journal prompts
- The boundary between real-world and FringeIsland stages is navigable in the studio
