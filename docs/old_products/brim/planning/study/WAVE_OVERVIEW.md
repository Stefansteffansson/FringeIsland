# Wave 5 — Brim (Horizon)

> *You stand at the edge. Beyond is everything.*

## Mission

Introduce the Void — the liminal space between the real world and FringeIsland.
Expand journeys to span all three dimensions. Give members the ability to shape
their own Garden. Launch the first mobile apps in beta. Brim is where the
FringeIsland universe becomes fully three-dimensional.

---

## Wave Is Done When

- [ ] The Void is defined and experientially accessible
- [ ] The Whisp's behaviour across all three dimensions is fully implemented
- [ ] Journey Studio v.3 supports cross-world journeys in limited testing
- [ ] FringeIsland Studio v.2 allows members to customise their Garden
- [ ] Dreamineers can begin developing the Void (testing and experimental)
- [ ] iOS mobile app v.01 is in beta with limited functionality and limited members
- [ ] Android mobile app v.01 is in beta with limited functionality and limited members
- [ ] All open questions for this wave are closed
