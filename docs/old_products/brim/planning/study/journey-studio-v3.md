# Journey Studio v.3

**Wave:** Brim
**Category:** Features
**Status:** 🔴 Needs concept work

---

## What Is This

Version 3 of Journey Studio introduces limited testing of journeys that span
all three dimensions — real world, the Void, and FringeIsland. This is the
first time a single journey can move a member through the full universe.

---

## Why We Are Building This

The full three-dimensional journey is the ultimate expression of the FringeIsland
experience. v.3 begins testing this capability in a controlled way before it
becomes available to all Dreamineers and members.
