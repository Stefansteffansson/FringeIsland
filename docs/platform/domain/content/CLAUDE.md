## What makes this entity different

DS-4 owns the **renderable substance of the universe** — media and assets with renditions, narrative content blocks, the capture → refine pipeline state, rendering contracts, retirement cascades, and Mist-capture ephemerality — never world placement (DS-1), story structure (DS-2), journey structure or delivery composition (DS-3), profile media (PC-substrate), or the renderers themselves (surfaces + design system). **DS-4 is the owned side of the standing opaque-reference pattern**: DS-1 places assets by ID, DS-2 beats and DS-3 steps reference blocks by ID — DS-4 serves ID-resolution to every referrer and never calls one, and its schema carries no knowledge of where content is referenced. Its primary authority is **ADR-U025 + the 2026-06-05 product locks** (the Gimbal-capture → Hub-refine pipeline; equipment profiles) rather than a dedicated canonical core — the first Domain Service with that authority shape; the narrative core supplies the block boundary. **Zero DS-4 substrate exists in code** (verified at the 2026-06-10 descent, Step 2): work here is greenfield against the spec, with two classified non-DS-4 look-alikes to not absorb (below).

## Rules that only apply at this entity

- **The eight service-level invariants in the spec's §7 are architecture, not features.** Opaque-reference direction (owned side — never call a referrer, never require referrer knowledge); the equipment-keying law (renditions are data served by variant; no DS-4 capability keys on equipment, ADR-U025); non-closure (asset/block/rendition kinds and pipeline states are registries, never sealed enums); Mist-capture ephemerality including blobs (ADR-U031); cascade-spec'd tombstoned retirement (ADR-U016 — retired IDs resolve to a defined tombstone, never an error that strands a renderer); capture personal-by-default with publication an explicit gated act; content-never-structure; i18n/a11y-capable shapes from day one. A feature spec or migration violating one fails review.
- **The write-path is studio-mode + scope gated, and DS-4 is not a fourth studio (ADR-U026 locked).** Authoring writes are gated by the role template of the mode the author stands in (Creator/Anthropologist from World Studio, Teller from Arc, Wayfinder from Journey — `has_permission()` against TEXT-keyed templates, D7) plus scope (personal-scope capture open to every FIM — the gate-by-scope law). Each studio's *structural* write target stays its own service; renderable payloads land here.
- **The content-boundary cluster is resolved (2026-06-10) — don't re-derive it.** Beats and steps reference DS-4 blocks opaquely by ID (DS-2 §8 Q8, DS-3 §8 Q7); the mythology three-way puts world-fact in DS-1, story-canon in DS-2, everything renderable here (DS-2 §8 Q3 + DS-1 §8 Q5).
- **Storage blobs are never more readable than their owning rows.** Buckets/policies follow PC-1 object-storage conventions and mirror row-grain RLS; bucket provisioning belongs in migrations, not the dashboard (the realized `avatars` bucket is the counter-example, routed to PC-1).
- **The block-kind vocabulary is gated on the ADR-U008 step-type specification session** (spec §8 Q1 — the session now carries the block-kind axis alongside DS-3's three step vocabularies); the capture foot is gated on the §8 Q3 real-world-rights privacy posture (research-spike candidate).

## Gotchas

- **`journeys.content` JSONB is not DS-4 substrate.** DS-3's realized table embeds renderable step content inline — classified (DS-4 Step 2, 2026-06-10) as DS-3 evolution debt predating the partition; the forward shape is steps referencing DS-4 blocks by ID. Don't read it as a DS-4 floor and don't build block storage inside it.
- **Profile/avatar media is not DS-4 territory.** `groups.avatar_url` + `AvatarUpload.tsx` + the `avatars` bucket are PC-2/PC-3 identity-presentation substrate (spec §8 Q2, classified 2026-06-10 — the realized RLS comments say the same). DS-4 owns *experience* content; don't migrate avatars here.
- **`content` is the noisiest term in the repo** (CSS/React usage, `journeys.content`, "intermediate" matches `media` loosely). Sweeps over DS-4 vocabulary need word boundaries, casing variants, and second-method verification of every zero — see the spec's §L3 Step 2 scoping record.
- **A Mist's captures are personal data with a TTL** — any capture-touching table joins the PC-2 ephemerality sweep and the atomic transcendence migration (cascade-spec'd jointly with PC-2 per ADR-U016 before build), and blob erasure in the storage substrate is part of the sweep's obligation, not just row deletion.
