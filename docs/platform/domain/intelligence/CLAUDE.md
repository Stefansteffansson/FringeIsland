## What makes this entity different

DS-7 owns the **Whisp as a being** — per the Whisp-split decision (DS-1 owns the world-presence face: cord, dial, severance, respawn; DS-7 owns the being face: dialogue, empty-fills-by-growth, the dissolved-assessment senses model, the maturity/internalisation arc, guard-railing enforcement) — plus **consent-gated profile accumulation** (ADR-U005's bucket/source shape) and **starved-drive sensing** (S28: which of Live/Grow/Matter is starved; lean the journey toward balance). DS-7 sits at the **top of the Domain dependency order**: it consumes (potentially) every sibling and **nothing consumes DS-7** — every outbound shaping flow goes through the *target service's own contract* (salience → DS-1, personalisation input → DS-3, generation through DS-4's gated write-path). The world never depends on the intelligence. **DS-7 is fully forward-committed**: zero realized substrate, all fourteen capabilities full-forward (verified at the descent, every zero dual-method). It is the only Domain Service whose authority chain carries a constitutional document: [`PRINCIPLES-AI.md`](../../../ecosystem/PRINCIPLES-AI.md).

## Rules that only apply at this entity

- **The ten service-level invariants in the spec's §7 are architecture, not features.** The load-bearing ones: one Whisp per person from the start, Shadow or FIM — never a second, never pooled, never transferred; **filling derives only from the FIM's own growth and disclosure** — never pre-filled, bought, or seeded from someone else's data; **no coercion surface exists anywhere** (disclosure motivation is intrinsic; personalisation deepens only with voluntary disclosure breadth); **instruments never surface as questionnaires** (S17 — structure preserved beneath dialogue, form transformed); the Whisp's internal state is **private to its FIM** (Stewards never see developmental data; no aggregates from private journeys); the **anti-leaderboard guardrail binds DS-7's outputs** (drive-balance readings, senses states, filling are never comparative across people). A feature spec or migration violating one fails review.
- **Guard rails are always human-authored; DS-7 enforces them — bidirectionally — and never authors them** (PRINCIPLES-AI, constitutional: if AI defined its own rails, authorship of the constraints would be handed to the thing being constrained). Any design where DS-7 generates, adjusts, or relaxes a rail fails review outright.
- **AI generation writes through DS-4's role-gated write-path with a distinct audit posture — no dedicated generation seam exists** (resolved 2026-06-11, joint `content.md` §8 Q8 / `journeys.md` §8 Q3). Generation acts under a human's authority, is attributable as AI-generated, and never ships unreviewed.
- **The Whisp never carries FIM-to-FIM communication** (resolved 2026-06-11, `communication.md` §8 Q5) — the branch is the FIM-FIM channel; dialogue is the being-face, not a transport.
- **Profile signals reach DS-6 only under explicit bucket-level PC-2 consent — never silently** (resolved 2026-06-11, `discovery.md` §8 Q2; declared interests only at Ferd).
- **Built to graduate, not retain:** the internalisation arc's endgame is DS-7's own recession. Any feature optimising for engagement-retention against the arc fails the canon (S6-7).

## Gotchas

- **"Profile" carries three senses on disk.** DS-7's profile *accumulation* (developmental data, ADR-U005's unrealized `profile_data`); PC-2's user-profile *record* (`lib/types/user.ts` `ProfileData` is profile-DISPLAY furniture — full_name/bio/avatar_url — a pure name collision); and ADR-U025's *equipment* profiles. Don't let a grep for one sense collect the others.
- **"Senses" carries two senses.** The Whisp's Big-5-mapped senses model (beings core, S17-18) vs the Gimbal as "the senses surface" (ADR-U025: camera/LiDAR/GPS). Different concepts; keep prose unambiguous.
- **The realized `assessment` step type is NOT DS-7 substrate and is in canon-tension.** `lib/types/journey.ts` carries `StepType = 'content' | 'activity' | 'assessment'` with questionnaire-form seeded steps (sprint1 + `seeds/05_professional_pathfinders.sql`) — exactly the form S17 transforms away. It is DS-3/ADR-U008 step-type territory, recorded as evolution debt at the DS-7 descent; the forward shape is the dissolved-instrument engine. Don't absorb it into DS-7; don't build new questionnaire surfaces against it.
- **Model-provider credentials and model-call plumbing are app-tier** (PC-1 Finding #4's channel) — never database-tier, never a DS-7 table. The dialogue-runtime substrate is an open question (spec §8 Q1); don't pre-commit it in migrations.
- **Shadow-Whisp data is TTL-bound** (ADR-U027): Shadows have their own Whisp from the start; their being-face data (dialogue state, accumulation) erases on inactivity, and transcendence carries it over atomically (S46). Accumulation features must handle both lifecycles from day one.
- **The validity question is open at canon level** (spec §8 Q2; the register row carries it): whether dissolved instruments retain psychometric validity is not resolvable in code — don't close it in a feature spec; the unwritten `whisp.md` canon sub-page is "prerequisite to DS-7 Intelligence implementation" (beings core).
