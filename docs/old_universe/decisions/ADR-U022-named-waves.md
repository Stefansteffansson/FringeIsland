# ADR-022 — Platform evolution — Named waves

> Extracted from `ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md` on 2026-04-05
> Amended 2026-04-06: Extended from two named waves to six — the full saga arc

**Status:** Locked
**Date:** March 2026 (original), April 2026 (amendment)

**Context:**
The platform evolves in waves, not hard-sequential phases. Each wave needed a name that connects it to the vision without overpromising its current state. The original decision named Wave 1 (Ferd) and Wave 2 (Hamn). As the project vision matured, the full arc was defined — six named waves forming a complete Old Norse saga.

**Decision:**
The platform evolves through six named waves. Each name is Old Norse/Swedish, drawn from the language of journeys, horizons, and deep mythology. Together they form a narrative arc — not just a release sequence, but a story of setting out, navigating, arriving, belonging, reaching the edge, and touching something foundational.

| Wave | Name | Meaning | The arc |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Ferd** | Journey, departure | You set out |
| 2 | **Eid** | Passage, crossing | You navigate the passage |
| 3 | **Hamn** | Harbour | You find harbour |
| 4 | **Heim** | Home | You arrive home |
| 5 | **Brim** | Edge, surf, horizon | You stand at the edge, gazing outward |
| Beyond | **Urd** | Fate, origin, what has become | You touch something older than the journey itself |

**Why names matter:**
Names carry intent. "Phase 1" carries no meaning beyond sequencing. "Ferd" — departure — correctly communicates that the current platform is the beginning of a journey, not the destination. Each subsequent name deepens the metaphor: Eid acknowledges that growth requires crossing difficult terrain. Hamn is arrival — the moment FringeIsland becomes a real harbour. Heim goes further — not just arrival but belonging, home. Brim is the edge of the known — standing at the horizon, looking outward. And Urd is the deepest layer — named for the Norn of the past, keeper of Urdarbrunnr (the Well of Fate) at the root of Yggdrasil. Beyond the horizon lies not nothing, but something ancient and foundational.

The sequence has a full mythological shape — a saga that ends not with arrival but with depth. Urd as the final tier suggests permanence, foundation, deep record — the immutable bedrock everything else builds on.

**Wave model vs phases:**
Phases imply sequential completion. Waves imply overlap — Ferd continues evolving as Eid begins, Eid work may start before Ferd ships. This is more honest about how software actually develops. The six-wave structure does not mean six sequential releases — it means six named horizons that the platform grows toward, each beginning before the previous is complete.

**Amendment history:**
- **March 2026:** Original decision — Ferd (Wave 1) and Hamn (Wave 2) established as named waves. Future releases to be named when they arrive.
- **April 2026:** Full saga arc defined. Wave 2 renamed from Hamn to Eid. Hamn moved to Wave 3. Three new waves added: Heim (Wave 4), Brim (Wave 5), Urd (beyond). Work package redistribution across new wave boundaries to follow separately.
